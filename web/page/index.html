The field

Two moulds of 400 walkers, yours on the left and the rival's on the right, grow over 23 food piles. A pile feeds 40 at a time, split by head count, and well-fed walkers win fights. More food lands every 15 s from 0:20.

Your tithe sends part of every bite to your bank instead of your mould. Spend it on beacons, spores, rallies and habits: pick one below the dish, then click a pile.

The rival you pick below starts buying within seconds. Pause (P) stops the clock, and you can still buy while it is stopped.
