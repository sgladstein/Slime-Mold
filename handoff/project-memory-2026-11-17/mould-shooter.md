On 2026-11-11 00:18Z the owner asked for a brainstorm of a demo where the mould is the enemy: a soldier or squad shoots it, and both sides need food. The design is in `Reports/mould-shooter-2026-11-11.md` and wiki `wiki/rts/shooter.md` (PR #38). It is design only, with no runs.

The lead idea is Larder. The player's stored food smells, so a hoard draws the mould. Shots work by holding a tube clear, because kills heal (the knife). The score is food shipped out over a fixed round. Four strategies (Fortress, Hunter, Farmer, Baiter) are meant to counter each other through whether the mould is lean (tree-shaped) or fat (looped).
