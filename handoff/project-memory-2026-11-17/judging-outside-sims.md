Judge other people's slime-mould simulations by what is interesting to watch and could become play, not by how much they look like the real organism (owner, 2026-11-10: "our goal is not a realistic slime mold. It is interesting simulations that we can turn into a game").

**Why:** a report called one Jenson picture "the only one that looks like real mould" and said its network was "no bigger than ours"; the owner disagreed on both. The second claim came from the GIF's first frame only; the full clip grows from 3-5 loops to 180-340.

**How to apply:** step through every frame of a GIF (contact sheet of evenly spaced frames) before describing it; frame them as "what happens / why it is interesting for a game". See [[research-source-access]].
