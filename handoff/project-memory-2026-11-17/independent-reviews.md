When the owner asks for an independent review agent, they want to see the prompt before it starts (2026-11-11 15:08Z). On 15:14Z they changed a numbers-check prompt: "the primary goal should be bigger picture. Did you miss something in your tests? Is your overall approach good? What other directions should be explored. I want it to tell us how to improve your work, not just validate a report."

Also: "biological realism should be an inspiration, not fully ignored". Fun over fidelity still holds, but a brief must not tell an agent to ignore biology.

**Why:** a review that only validates numbers can't find a wrong approach or a missed direction.

**How to apply:** lead the review brief with these points:
1. Is the approach right for the goal?
2. What did the tests miss (controls, confounds)?
3. Biology and literature as inspiration.
4. Ranked next directions, each with a first experiment.

Correctness checks come after these. See [[judging-outside-sims]] and [[brainstorm-briefs]].
