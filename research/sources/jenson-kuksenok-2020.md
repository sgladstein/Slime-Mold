# Jenson & Kuksenok 2020: the Dismantling performance

A stylus on a tablet drives a Jones-based simulation live. Video:
https://youtu.be/hEbbmJaYHAc (not watched).

## Key facts for us

- Stylus position and pressure spawn particles and change parameters.
- "Additional feedback loops were incorporated into the model to increase
  the heterogeneity of the patterns."
- A "critical particulate density" gives a tipping point, a phase shift over
  several minutes.
- No numbers, no code.
