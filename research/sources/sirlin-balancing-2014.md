# Balancing Multiplayer Games, Parts 1-3 (Definitions; Viable Options; Fairness)

Balance is viable options plus fairness of starting options. Yomi layers 0-3:
each move needs a counter; past layer 3 the first move returns. A dominant move
is the worst outcome. Local imbalance between units is fine; races must be fair.
Tier-list process: remove god tier, lift garbage tier, compress; start new moves
too strong (Pardo); avoid matchup special cases. Cites Pardo that RTS super
weapons are generally bad and the StarCraft nuke's built-in weaknesses (Ghost,
red dot, countdown). Checkmates "can be ok"; lame-duck endings are not.

Quotes: "The worst thing you can have in a competitive multiplayer game is a
dominant move"; "The Yomi Layer concept is a reminder that moves need to have
counters."; "all the theory in the world does not substitute for playtesting."
