# The making of Company of Heroes: prototypes, design and the 'Donkeyschreck'

Nine to ten months of prototype in the Impossible Creatures engine; almost no
tuning carried to Essence. Influence territory from buildings at first; fixed
sectors came "like nine months from the end". "one of the biggest decisions in
Essence was directional cover and flanking". Range matters in combat; pinning
gives time to react; retreat and veterancy keep squads alive.
