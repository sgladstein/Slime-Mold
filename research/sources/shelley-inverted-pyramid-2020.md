# Soundbite: Bruce Shelley's 'inverted pyramid of decision making'

Start with one unit and one decision, and let decisions multiply. "you start
the game with one unit and you make a decision with it and then that opens up a
door." Also: "you had 15 minutes to engage somebody or you'd lose them."
