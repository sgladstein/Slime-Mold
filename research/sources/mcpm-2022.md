# MCPM 2022

Jones's model made stochastic and 3D, fitted to galaxy data (the NASA
cosmic-web story).

## Key facts for us

- Direction, distance and a branch/mutation decision are sampled; the
  branch chance is d1^s / (d0^s + d1^s) with "sampling exponent" s. As s
  grows it becomes Jones's deterministic rule.
- No per-cell limit; a separate trace field records equilibrium density.
- 10M agents on 1024^3 grids, about 150-200 ms a step on a TitanX; 100M at
  about 300 ms. C++ and DirectX compute.
- Typical: sensing angle about twice the move angle; sensing distance about
  ten times the move distance; exponent 2.2-5.
- Attractive agents make stable nets; repulsive ones transient cells.
- Cites Jenson & Kuksenok 2020 (Fig. 11 caption).
