# Synchronous RTS Engines 2: Sync Harder

From Gas Powered Games (Supreme Commander): the sim runs at a fixed 10 ticks a
second, the user layer up to 60 fps; only input is sent and runs on a future
tick. Replays and rejoin re-simulate the input: "The input data is tiny".
"you could just fix the desyncs" rather than build recovery.
