# GDC 2012: Sid Meier on how to see games as sets of interesting decisions

Interesting decisions involve trade-offs, are situational ("Good decisions are
situational."), persist, and come with enough information. Risk against reward,
short against long term. "probably a third of the things that we try... end up
getting taken out of the game"
