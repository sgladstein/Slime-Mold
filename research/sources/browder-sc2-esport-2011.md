# The Game Design of StarCraft II: Designing an E-Sport (GDC 2011 talk), via two write-ups

Second-hand: quotes are as the bloggers report them. 45 units and 3 sides
against Dawn of War's 60+ and Supreme Commander's 100+, so each unit does more:
Colossus climbs cliffs; Force Field protects, splits or funnels. Counter cycle:
Banelings beat Marines, Stim turns it, a Baneling upgrade turns it back. Units
kept smaller than buildings for clarity. "Micro-management is important: that's
the R of RTS." Standard openings exist but no standard build timing.
