# Total Annihilation (Wikipedia article)

Metal and energy stream: "accumulated at a constant rate rather than in small
batches", unlimited; overspending slows all building. A physics engine "governs
projectiles, explosions and wreckage"; height-map terrain blocks artillery.
Initially 250 units a side, later 500. Supreme Commander is its successor.
