# Ensemble's Shelley Explains 'Design By Playing' (GCDC 2007 talk coverage)

Plan, prototype quickly, playtest (a year or more), polish, publish. "Designers
are only guessing until they play"; "You must play to judge a game"; "A
multiplayer prototype existing at the six month mark is the goal". Play to
test, not to win. Ensemble's semi-compulsory multiplayer sessions ("Angry
Tuesdays"). "You can't iterate forever." Frequent, even daily, testing is the
article's paraphrase, not a quote.
