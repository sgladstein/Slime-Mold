# Designing victory conditions: Company of Heroes, Rise of Nations, Sins of a Solar Empire: Diplomacy

Against drawn-out mop-up endings. Company of Heroes' victory points (odd count;
first to zero loses), Rise of Nations' wonder countdown, Sins' diplomacy points.
"it automatically rules out the possibility of a grind, a slog, or a
stalemate"
