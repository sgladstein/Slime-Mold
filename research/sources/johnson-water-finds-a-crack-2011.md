# GD Column 17: Water Finds a Crack

Players optimise and find every exploit ("water finds a crack"). A dominant
strategy removes choice because "all other options are provably sub-optimal".
Civilization's infinite city sleaze was fixed in Civ IV by per-city maintenance
that scales with the number of cities. "Often, designers don't even understand
their own games until they finally see them in the wild." Not an RTS (4X).
