# Tooth and Tail interviews: 'Crafting Tooth & Tail, a gamepad RTS that aims to Hearthstone StarCraft' and 'Road to the IGF: Pocketwatch Games' Tooth and Tail'

"We wanted to take anything that felt like a chore in your classic RTS and dump
it." Camera tied to one commander, so attention is a resource. Same-looking
production buildings per tier: "enough information to make interesting
decisions, but not enough information to totally give away what the opponent is
doing." "Build orders get memorized and the game boils down to the player who
can execute more efficiently", hence procedural maps. Animal species make roles
readable.
