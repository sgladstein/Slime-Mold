# Real World Games Look Like Spinning Tops

Strategy spaces of real games (Tic-Tac-Toe, Go, StarCraft II among them) have
a transitive axis (strength) and a non-transitive one (the number of cycles at
each strength), shaped like a spinning top. Nash clustering measures it. "it
clarifies why populations of strategies are necessary for training of agents".
That cycles are widest at middling strength is the paper's figure as commonly
read; check the full text before quoting it as a number.
