# Jenson, physarum (2019)

The site refuses automated readers (robots.txt): use the saved copy.

## Key facts for us

- Jones 2010 with two changes: no one-particle-per-cell rule, and contagion
  ("When a particle is infected, it lays down an infected trail which then
  spreads to other particles").
- C++/GLSL in openFrameworks, all on the GPU, real time on a GTX 1070, "most
  of the examples comprised of between 5 and 10 million particles".
- No parameter values given; no code published; the promised CPU version
  never appeared.
- network_2.gif grows from 3-5 loops to 180-340 in about 9 s
  (`../../Reports/jenson-physarum-2026-11-10/gif_frames.py`).
