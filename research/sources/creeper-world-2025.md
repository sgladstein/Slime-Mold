# Crafting Creeper World: An Interview with Knuckle Cracker (also as 'Crafting the Creep of Creeper World' on Game-Wisdom)

"I wanted to simplify strategy gaming and also explore a new type of RTS
enemy... based on a fluid simulation." Later games run the creeper over terrain
"controlled by a falling sand simulator". Maps with "more than one possible path
to victory" and safe areas; forgiving maps instead of a difficulty slider.
