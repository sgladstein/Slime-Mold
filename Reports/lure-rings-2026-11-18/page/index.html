The Lure shows its reach as thin cream rings

A Lure pulls the calm slime toward it, round rock and walls, so the turrets and walls beside it make a kill zone. The lab's prototype drew that pull in pink with a haze over it, and the Lure lit a green pool of its own, so the place you are meant to shoot into was washed over. Now the pull is drawn as thin cream rings and nothing else. Each pair below is one game, walker for walker.

What to look for

  • The rings are the pull itself. Each ring is 10 cells further to walk from the Lure, so they bend round rock and walls and stop where the pull stops. The calm slime leans across them toward the middle. They stand still: a ring that drifted inward would be drawn on, not the game.
  • Brightest at the Lure, fainter outward, and over slime they drop to a third, so the slime stays the thing you read.
  • The orange at the Lure is the turrets' shots landing. It is the flush from the flinch look that has just gone in. Under the green pool it could not be seen.
  • A charge ignores the Lure. Panicking, surging and raiding slime and the mini-bosses do not lean to it, so the rings show where the calm body goes, not where a rush goes.
  • The colour is your team's cream. The lab's pink is kept for the core planet's plants. Green rings were tried and were lost on the meadow. The Lure's own light was tried in cream and read as a pale mist over the fight, so while the rings show it is not lit.

In the game

Not yet. These clips are filmed on the lab's prototype of the pull, at pull 10. The rings are built into the game's picture and wait on two pieces: the Enemy lane's pull as a real rule, and the Squad lane's Lure building in the run shop. When both are in, a Lure you build shows its rings with nothing to switch.
