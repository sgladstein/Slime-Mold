Multicast

A new card makes one shot fire two cards at once. Multicast fires the card after it beside its own, in the same shot; a second multicast adds a third. Every clip is the game window's own frames on software Vulkan (the Fork, eight soldiers holding at the larder, squad mode). All clips are one game up to 29 s with plain bullets, the same start as the trigger clips. At 29 s every squad loads the clip's cards, so the clips differ only in those cards over the next 3 s.

How it works

  • Multicast (cost 3, rare): each shot of its card's bullet also fires the next card, each as its own bullets, a little apart. That card leaves the gun's turn, so every shot fires both.
  • Two multicasts fire three cards in one shot.
  • With a trigger: multicast + trigger + plain + blast + gas fires a plain bullet carrying a blast, with a gas beside it.
  • What you see: two or three bullets leave the barrel together, and the gun-card strip lights every card the next shot fires.
Every clip loops. Slow motion shows the two bullets leaving each barrel together.

A blast in every shot

On the left the gun takes turns: a plain shot, then a blast. On the right every shot fires both, side by side.

Over 10 s from the same start: in turn, 31 blasts, 1,941 killed, health 70; multicast, 38 blasts from 77 fired, 2,018 killed, health 80. The extra blasts clear the near slime, and the rest fly on past it.

Combos

Frame cost

  • Bursts are now read back in batches. Before, the game waited on the GPU after every bomb's kill, every throw and every gas. Now it runs a step's bursts together and reads them back once. In the 10 s multicast + bomb + gas run, that was 69 waits instead of 207. Each burst still counts exactly what it did on its own, which the tests check.
  • Multicast adds bullets under the same cap as every other bullet, and no new GPU pass.
  • Not yet timed on the GTX 1070.

Not settled

  • Price: cost 3 and rare, like the plan's other rule cards. That is a guess for now; the balance pass will set it.
  • Copy and always, the plan's last rule cards, are not built.
