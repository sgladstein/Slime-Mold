Squad Movement Rework

Squads were clunky because every soldier dashed at full speed to its place and stopped dead, the squad's anchor stopped dead whenever it got ahead, figures were drawn only 15 times a second, and each soldier waited on its own walking map. Now each soldier has a velocity: it walks in step with its squad, slows into its place, queues or steps aside instead of shoving, and is drawn smoothly between game steps.

Each clip: main on the left, this change on the right, the same game, 60 frames a second. Judder is how far a drawn soldier's step differs from its last one, in screen pixels a frame (0 is a perfectly even walk); hitches are frames where a walking soldier froze or jumped.

"never" means not within the move's time (15 to 26 s). On planets 1 and 8 (seeds 1-6, 40 and 200 soldiers, 24 pairs), the new walk had less jerk in 23 of 24, less turning in 24 of 24, fewer walking-map waits in 24 of 24 and a lower mean cost in 21 of 24 (sign test p < 0.001 for each). Arrival time, pace and stand-still share did not change reliably. CPU cost on this cloud machine; the 1070 has not timed it.

Worse, and still open

  • A line settles a little after arriving: 1.1 s with 40 and 4.1 s with 200, where the old line stood at once. It now forms as it walks, and its last members step into place at the end.
  • Planet 1 with 200 now arrives on every seed (17.1 s, it never did), but takes 22.8 s to stand still afterwards against 14.8.
  • Big squads in caves are still the slowest case: planet 8 with 200 does not arrive within 26 s before or after, and a member stands still a third of the time while the squad moves.
  • A ring or wedge of 200 is one soldier deep, 160 cells across: it walks as a packed knot and spreads out once there. Rings in bands and a capped wedge are cheap to try.
  • The worst steps with 200 on planet 8 rose (p95 7.66 → 8.40 ms) while the mean fell; which part costs it is not measured yet.
  • Next big step (the independent review's first pick): hand out formation places fitted to the open ground, so a squad pours through a corridor and re-forms past it without the special cases.

Try it

Once the change is merged, double-click play.cmd: it pulls main and builds the game. The old walk is still there for comparison with set=steer=0.
