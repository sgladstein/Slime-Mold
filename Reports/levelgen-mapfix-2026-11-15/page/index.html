Planet Map Fixes

The first map fixes from the three planet reviews, looks first. Nests now sit off the map's edges and, on the big planets, spread across the map. The core world has fewer streams and at most two rivers. Rings round nests are broken arcs, hedges stay off water, bridges are straight decks and fords taper. Every picture pairs the reviewed build (before) with this change (after), same planet, same seed, with the counts beside it.

1

The core world: nests spread out, fewer streams

LooksPlayreview item 5

Before, two nests had to stand 280 map cells apart on every planet, so on the 6x core world they bunched in one corner. Now they stand 0.3 of the map's width apart. Streams grew with the map, which webbed the whole core world; now a bigger map keeps the normal count, and rivers stop at two.

2

Rings round nests are broken arcs

Looksreview item 5

A ring of hedge round a nest was one round C with one gap, and read as a bullseye. Now it is two or three arcs, each at its own distance from the nest, with a gap after each.

3

Hedges stay off water; nests stay off the edges

Looksreview item 10

A hedge piece is left out where either end or its middle is wet, so no more beige tubes laid across lakes. Nests keep at least 0.16 of the map's width from the sides and 0.2 of its height from the top and bottom (150-240 map cells), so the slime's starting blob isn't cut by the frame.

4

Straight bridges and tapered fords

Looksreview item 9

A bridge deck took the river's own ragged outline. It is now a straight band that runs 6 cells past the widest the banks reach. At a ford, the deep middle now narrows to it over 9 cells either side, instead of stopping square.

Not fixed here, and next

  • Cave gates on pools. Hedge pieces on water rose in the caves (164 → 217): they are gates across tunnels with a pool in them. A gate locks its pass, so it stays; it could move to the dry end of its tunnel.
  • River valley seed 2's near nest sits by the bridge, so the slime holds the bridge from the start. Keeping nests off river crossings goes with the next item.
  • Next: a front door and a back road for every nest. A direct way at most 10 wide and a way round at least 16 wide, checked when the planet is built (the play review found 4 of 112 nests with both). Then fens weighted to lakes, cave tunnels 5-15 wide, and a landing crystal per biome.
