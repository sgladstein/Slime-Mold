Bullet shapes

The first step of the gun-card plan: each bullet set does something of its own to the slime, blast and bomb stop being two sizes of one explosion, and the germ bullet goes on sale. Every clip is the game window's own frames on software Vulkan (the Fork, eight soldiers holding at the larder, squad mode). The paired clips are one game up to 29 s with plain bullets; at 29 s every squad loads the clip's bullet, so they differ only in what that bullet does over the next 3 s.

What changed

  • Slug: each hit shoves the slime around it away from the gun, out to 3 strike radii. It is a real throw of the walkers there (they land and live on), with no extra kill.
  • Needle: flies on through two more slime hits, so one shot makes a line of three small holes into the body.
  • Buckshot: each shot is four pellets in a fan, each half the radius.
  • Standard: as before.
  • A payload takes its set's shape: a needle blast blasts three times in a line, a buckshot gas is four small clouds, a slug bomb shoves and throws.
  • Blast holds, bomb throws. The blast is unchanged: it kills a 6-cell disc and its crater keeps the slime off for 20 s. The bomb now kills only 2.5 cells, throws the slime out to 10 cells away from the gun, and leaves its crater only 1 s. It is drawn ice blue instead of a second orange.
  • The germ bullet is sold from planet 4, like the bomb. It was held back because the GPU world couldn't cast it; it has since 11-14.
Every clip loops. Slow motion makes the pellets, the lance and the shove easy to follow.

Can you name the set?

These four are standard, slug, needle and buckshot, in a shuffled order, with standard numbers so only the shape differs. Reply in the thread with which clip you think is which. That is the test for this step: if the sets don't read apart by eye, the shapes need to be bolder.

The bomb before and after, a whole game of bombs

Bombs from the start, 30 to 33 s: today's main on the left, this change on the right. By 33 s the new bomb has killed about a third as much and thrown about 40% more.

A first version threw the slime all round the hit, as the old bomb did. With the longer throw the near half landed on the squad and its health at 33 s fell to 49, so the throw now goes away from the gun.

Not settled

  • The needle is strong. At standard numbers it killed 1,265 in these 3 s against a standard bullet's 347, because its second and third hits land in the thick of the body. Its price still comes only from its rolled numbers. This is a tuning note for the balance pass, per the build-first rule.
  • Slug and buckshot kill less (138 and 179). The slug trades kills for keeping the front back: over a whole game of slugs the squad ended at health 95 against 72 for standard bullets (one seed).
  • Frame cost on the GTX 1070 is not measured yet. Standard bullets run exactly as before. Buckshot is four rays a shot, and each slug hit adds one throw, which on the GPU is one pass over the walkers and one read-back, the same as a bomb's.
