# Plans: rush (the CI run bot), kite (the same, steering away from slime
# inside 0.8 of its range), turtle (pumps, turrets, walls, a lure; home

#
# Deciding measures, named before the run: soldiers lost (fell_end) and
# won, paired off/on by planet, seed and plan, sign test per plan. Also
# raids sent by aim (know=...:b/l/s/x) and lost_buildings. Kill: rush or
# kite lose fewer soldiers with the gate on 5 of 6 seeds on a planet; a
# home plan (turtle, econ) wins more or loses fewer buildings with it on;
# raids sent fall by half.
