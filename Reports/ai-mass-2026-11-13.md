# The player AI on the 25,000-walker mould (2026-11-13)

Integration's tuning note, from the Larder thread's walker-count audit (#199): the brain's walker thresholds (`Style::dare`, `Style::wall_alarm`, `HELD`) were set on the 5,000-walker mould and read whole walkers. Shape C, the default since #174, has 25,000 walkers weighing 0.2 each (`Rules::mass`), so those thresholds tripped at a fifth of the body's thickness. The AI judged crystals unsafe and held, and walled early.

**The change:** `View::weigh(n)` = n × `Rules::mass`. Each threshold now compares weighted walkers. At 5,000 walkers (mass 1) nothing changes: the census replays identically (seeds 5-6, boom, claim, fort, strike).

**Smoke run**, race 5-8 at 25,000 walkers (mass 0.2), main 6e9008a against the change, about 30 min on one machine. Raw output: [ai-mass-2026-11-13/](ai-mass-2026-11-13/).

| Hand | Score higher-lower | Harvest higher | Lost games, main → change |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| boom | 4-0 (+0.02 to +0.08) | 4 of 4 (28.8k → 42.3k on seed 5) | 0 → 0 |
| claim | 4-0 | 4 of 4 | 1 → 0 |
| strike | 1-3 | 4 of 4 | 3 → 3 |

- **The AI farms the crystals it used to fear.** Harvest rose on all 12 games, by 6k to 14k food.
- **The strike still dies on C.** Its squad is wiped on 3 of 4 either way. The Larder thread saw the same since #174 made walking into thick slime deadly, so it's the rush itself, not the thresholds.
- **Four seeds is a smoke check.** 4-0 is p 0.125, so nothing here is significant.
