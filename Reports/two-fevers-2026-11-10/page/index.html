Seed your fever

Click a tube to seed Ember. The walkers under the ring catch it, the fever runs along the tubes, and every walker it passes turns your colour and stays yours until Tide's fever takes it back. A spore pays for each seed; one grows back every 10 s, three at most. Your score is the share of the host you own, averaged over three minutes.

Spread. A carrier lays its fever's own field along the tube. A walker standing in enough of it catches the fever, so a fever runs along tubes at a few cells a second and dies out across bare agar.
Hold. After 4 s a carrier recovers and stays your colour. For 15 s it is held: neither fever can catch it. Then it is open to your rival, while your own fever cannot refresh it for 40 s. Deep colour is held ground, pale is ground that can be taken.
Hush. A hushed fever's carriers lay no trail, so the tubes under them thin and break, which can cut a road your rival's fever would run down. Your fever needs those tubes too. Switch it with the button or H; it can switch every 3 s.
