Grow, raid, seal

You start owning a patch of mould round the left end pile; Tide owns the right. Call your walkers to food: they build tubes there, and every walker of yours on a pile grows your spores faster. Seed to catch walkers with your fever; it runs down tubes, fastest down busy ones, and turns what it passes your colour. Pinch your own tube (free, once every 5 s) to cut a road the rival's fever is coming down, at the price of your own supply. Fed walkers you own resist the rival's fever; starving ones do not. Walkers of yours cut off from all food drift back to nobody's. Score: your share of the mould, averaged over three minutes.

The trunk dilemma. A call on a rich pile grows a thick tube to it: the best income you can have, and the fastest road for your rival's fever into your body. Many thin routes are safer and earn less.
Holding ground. Ground a fever has just taken is held for 15 s (deep colour); then the other fever can take it (pale). Your own fever cannot retake its ground for 40 s, so you defend by sealing, and by keeping your body fed: walkers you own on a tube route to food catch the rival's fever ten times less often.
Reading the rival. Tide's calls and spores are on screen. A thick blue tube forming toward a pile is Tide's income and your best target; a pale blue patch is ground you can take now.
