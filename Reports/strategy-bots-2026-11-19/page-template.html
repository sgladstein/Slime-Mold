How the bots play

A bot's plan is a family plus dials, written as one line, such as econ,go=4,aim=6. The family sets a starting style and the dials override it. Every second the bot spends its points by the plan, then picks what the squad does: guard home, raise a building, pump or drain a crystal, or hunt the heaviest slime it can shoot. Between those turns it re-aims the volley as often as the aim dial says. The search keeps the best plans, changes one or two dials on each to make new ones, and plays the new ones on every map.

{{FAMILY_ROWS}}
The eight families
go
How many soldiers before the squad leaves home.
aim
How many times a second the squad re-aims at the heaviest slime in range. You aim about 3 times a second, 6.6 in your big push.

What to build at home, and how many pumps get turrets round them.
more, take

What the points buy between planets, and whether to buy the card the next planet asks for.
kite
Back away from slime that gets this close, as a share of the gun's range.
split, group
Your crystal-holding play: send groups of this many soldiers to drain and hold one crystal each.
want
Gun cards to buy when the run offers them, such as heave or the gun lab's seek/wide/blast.needle.
mop
Once the slime is down to this many walkers, stop building and draining and hunt every last scrap.

Is the search learning?

Overnight, the search scored plans on planets 1 to 3 together, 974 games in three generations. It learned to clear planets 1 and 2 faster with rush plans, and planet 3 wins fell from 6 of 128 games to none. That is what you predicted: speed on planet 1 picks strategies that fail on planet 3.

Since 14:30 UTC on 19 November the search plays planet 3 only. Two changes copied from your play log made the biggest gains: aiming several times a second instead of once, and leaving home at once instead of waiting for 40 soldiers. Before them the best plan won 3 of 9 maps in about 10 minutes. With them it wins 3 of 9 in 6:20 and comes close on more. The utility bot, whose dials are 40 weights, won none of 192 planet 3 games, so the search dropped it.
