Trigger cards

Two new cards make a bullet carry the card after it. Trigger lets that card out where the bullet hits slime; Expire lets it out where the bullet stops. The carried card leaves the gun's own turn, so every shot fires both. Every clip is the game window's own frames on software Vulkan (the Fork, eight soldiers holding at the larder, squad mode). All clips are one game up to 29 s with plain bullets; at 29 s every squad loads the clip's cards, so they differ only in those cards over the next 3 s.

How they work

  • Trigger: at the bullet's first slime hit, the carried card is fired from there as its own bullets, straight on along the same line. A carried buckshot is four pellets, a needle lances on, and a slug shoves. The bullet's hit first clears a hole, so the carried bullet flies through that hole and goes off at the next slime: right at the hit, or deeper in.
  • Expire: the same, but where the bullet stops. That is after its last hit (a needle's third), at the end of its range (where it flies on), or at a wall (where it bounces back off the wall's face).
  • Chains: a carried card can carry one more, so a gun can relay a shot two steps deep.
  • What you see: a carrier has a bead of the carried card's colour at its head, and the gun-card strip lights the carrier and its cargo together as the next shot.
Every clip loops. Slow motion makes the carried bullets easy to follow out of each hit.

A blast in every shot

The same cards with and without a trigger. On the left the gun takes turns: a plain shot, then a blast. On the right every shot is a plain bullet carrying a blast, which goes off at the next slime past the hole it made.

Over 10 s from the same start (29 to 39 s, 80 shots): without the trigger, 31 blasts, 1,941 killed, health 70; with it, 24 blasts from 41 let out, 2,037 killed, health 76. The trigger's early burst cleared the front, so fewer plain bullets found slime after that.

Not settled

  • Buckshot with a trigger is strong: 42 blasts in 3 s against 8 for plain and blast taking turns. That is the kind of lucky combo the plan is after, so the balance pass can price it later.
  • A trigger alone adds little over 10 s (2,037 killed against 1,941). What it changes is where the payload goes off and what it pairs with.
  • Frame cost on the GTX 1070 is not measured. A carried bullet is an ordinary bullet in flight, under the same cap as every other. Each bomb or slug shove still costs one GPU read-back, which the plan's batching step covers.
