A raid that leaves as one body

A raid now gathers inside the slime, walks out as one body down its way round the rocks, and pours onto its target, or onto your squad if it meets it on the way. Slime breaking away to a new crystal keeps touching its neighbours instead of spraying off as single specks.

2026-11-18, the Enemy lane. From the owner's play notes that day: "Often, especially during raids the slime feels more like a bunch of individual walkers instead of a single slime unit ... Seeing the individual walkers doesn't bother me when I am attacking a big slime blob and the walkers surge towards me, but when they all leave the blob and most of what I see is individual walkers that is problematic."
