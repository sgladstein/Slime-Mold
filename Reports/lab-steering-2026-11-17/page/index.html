Recommendation

Build it into the game, changed.

It is the first thing the player owns that writes a direction and not damage. It reads at a glance, and it turns the walls and turrets already in the game into a plan. It also meets the condition set for the lure coming back to the run shop: it visibly draws the slime.

  • Start at pull 10, not 30. At 30, a lure and three turrets empty a planet 1 home colony in about 45 seconds for no losses. That is a win button.
  • Make how hard a slime follows a trait of its tier. A Trunk that barely leans and a Swarmer that stampedes are different planets from one number.
  • Keep the lure mortal. As a building, the slime ate it 28 seconds after it went on. That is a fair clock.
  • Give the pusher a carrier. A dart or a canister, used with a wall or a lure.
  • The ring colour is a placeholder. This pink sits in the hue kept for the core planet's plants.

Not tried yet: a gun that lays the pull along its shots, leapfrogging lures to walk a body across the map, and planets past 3.

Do not build a bullet that lays the pull. Deliver the lure by aim instead.

A bullet lands where the slime is nearest the squad, so marks laid by every hit gather round the squad, not where the player wants the slime to go. No version beat plain fire on kills. Pushes make the body back off the squad's fire (fewer kills, more slime alive). Placement is what herds: part one's lure, put down between the turrets, drew the home colony off its crystal and into them. In this scene the squad already stood in range, so even aimed darts added only 12 to 20% to the turrets' food.

  • The gun's job is to carry the lure. A lure dart, or a canister thrown where the player aims, is the same lure as the building and needs nothing new from the slime.
  • A lead worth one more look: evicting starves. A strong pull that drags a colony off its crystal shrinks it without killing it. That is another way to win a planet, and it fits the Lure now going in.

Part three: the dart

The gun carries the lure. The squad fires a dart where the player aims, up to 60 cells away, over slime and walls. Build the dart as the Lure's second form, and give it a pusher setting.

At the game's pull of 10 a dart every 10 seconds takes a crystal off a colony in 30 to 45 seconds and leaves the planet's slime about a fifth smaller by 2:00. It costs kills: the squad has less in front of it to shoot, and only part of the body crosses the turrets, so they render about half the food. The trade is a crystal now against food over time, which is a real choice.

  • Lure or pusher. The lure dart is quicker and needs the turrets in its line. The pusher dart works from the near side, so it fits a squad that cannot reach behind the slime. Both together did no better than either.
  • Pull 30 adds nothing here. The slime ends the same size and the turrets get less. The darts land close to the colony, so the game's pull of 10 is already enough.
  • What I set and did not tune: 60 cells of range, a 10 second life, one dart every 10 seconds. A price and a cooldown are Direction's to set.
