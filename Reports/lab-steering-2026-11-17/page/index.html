Recommendation

Build it into the game, changed.

It is the first thing the player owns that writes a direction and not damage. It reads at a glance, and it turns the walls and turrets already in the game into a plan. It also meets the condition set for the lure coming back to the run shop: it visibly draws the slime.

  • Start at pull 10, not 30. At 30, a lure and three turrets empty a planet 1 home colony in about 45 seconds for no losses. That is a win button.
  • Make how hard a slime follows a trait of its tier. A Trunk that barely leans and a Swarmer that stampedes are different planets from one number.
  • Keep the lure mortal. As a building, the slime ate it 28 seconds after it went on. That is a fair clock.
  • Give the pusher a carrier. A dart or a canister, used with a wall or a lure.
  • The ring colour is a placeholder. This pink sits in the hue kept for the core planet's plants.

Not tried yet: a gun that lays the pull along its shots, leapfrogging lures to walk a body across the map, and planets past 3.
