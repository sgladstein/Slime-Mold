A bite lights only what it bites

When the slime bites a soldier, a brief hot light shows on the side it bit from. That light reached 3.5 dish cells, more than twice as far as a bite does, and a soldier wading in is bitten many times a second, so a squad in the slime stood in a flickering orange pool five times the ground its bites cover. Now the light reaches as far as the bite itself, the soldier's touch of 1.5 cells: each bite is a small hot glow on its own side, and the figures and the slime round them keep their own colours. Each pair is one game drawn two ways, the Fork held at 150,265 with plain rounds, at the game's own pace.

The other lights

Every light the fight makes was checked against the ground its damage covers. A shot's light reaches as far as its hit, a burst's as far as its blast, a flare's half its alarm's reach, and the muzzle flash and a waiting round's ember one cell. Only the bite's was bigger than its damage.
