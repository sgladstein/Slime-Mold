# The gun range (2026-11-15)

The owner, 02:55Z: "I need a test environment built where I can play with
the guns and cards and bullets."

## What was built

**Gun range** is a new row on the start menu, under Random planet. It opens
an armory first. "Back to the range" then starts the sandbox's round (the
map and options as Sandbox would play them, shape C slime), with the squad
armed from the armory.

- **The armory is a run** (`Run::range(seed)`, its `range` flag set), so the shop
  page and all of the run's gun building are reused: slots fired left to
  right, drag onto a slot, drag onto the bag, Q and E.
  - Nothing has a price, and the shelf never sells out (`Run::buy`).
  - There are no upgrades or planets. Its depth only sets the rolls
    (`RANGE_DEPTH` 3 to start).
- **Guns.** Each kind is owned at its average. The rack has one rolled gun
  of each kind. Clicking a rack gun swaps it with the owned gun of its kind,
  so there are always four of each, and you can swap back.
- **Bullets.** The shelf has every payload in every set (`Shell::ALL` ×
  `Set::ALL`). The standard set is exact (`Ammo::standard`); the others are
  rolled for the depth (`Ammo::roll_of`). After them come every card that is
  not a bullet. The shop groups them as Bullets, Cards for the next bullet,
  Cards for every bullet, and In the bag (`menu::range_shelf`).
- **Tiles on the left:** New rolls, Deeper rolls, Shallower rolls, Empty the
  gun, Empty the bag. These are the picks `DevReroll`, `DevDepth(±1)`,
  `EmptyGun` and `EmptyBag`, which the range allows without dev mode.
- **The range round** (`Args::range`, also `range=SEED` on the command line):
  - no time limit: `RANGE_CAP` is ten hours, and the clock shows only the
    time played;
  - the bank starts at 100,000 (`RANGE_BANK`), so B always buys a soldier;
  - the objective line reads "Gun range: H opens the armory, B buys soldiers,
    N a new map";
  - squad 0 is armed with the armory's gun (`Run::carried`), and the army
    round holds its cards;
  - Restart and N keep the gun.
- **H in the range** (Shift+H and Ctrl+H too, and the loadout strip's
  button) opens the armory over the live round and pauses it
  (`App::open_armory`).
  - Back to the range, Esc or a right click closes it (`App::close_armory`):
    every squad gets the gun (`Cmd::Arm`) and goes back on the army round,
    which now holds the new cards (`Cmd::Load(q, None)`). The same round
    carries on.
  - The view's own minimap is switched off while the armory is up, because
    the camera stays zoomed in (`App::set_armory`).
- **Leave the range** goes to the main page. The armory is kept, so the gun
  is still built the next time.

## Film

`larder-world look=DIR range=7 menu=shop hand=stand screen=1600x900
centre=256,140,4.5 at=0,0.01..0.47,5.0,5.1..11.0
drag=Carbine>Carbine/Standard blast>1/Wide>1/Split>3/go`. The new `go` drag
token closes the armory at the film's first whole second. A drag from a gun
to itself clicks it (`StartMenu::card_at` now finds gun cards too, the last
of a name, which is the rack's). GIFs come from `raw/make_gifs.py`, and the
log is in `raw/film-log.txt`.

- **Armory:** the rack's carbine was swapped in. Then Standard blast went to
  slot 1, Wide in front of it, and Split to slot 3. That fills 3 of 6 slots,
  budget 7 of 8.
- **Fight** (Fork seed 1, 4 soldiers, `hand=stand`): 44 shots and 396
  walkers killed by 5 s, then 103 shots and 816 killed by 11 s. The slime
  went from 25,000 to 24,208. The slime comes up the stem to the larder, so
  the wide blasts land near the squad. The pale discs are the craters.
- In the film the round runs on while the armory is up, by a few hundredths
  of a second. Paused, `App::step` holds still, and the film's first try
  hung there. In the game the armory pauses the round.

Pictures and GIFs are in `gun-range-2026-11-15/`.

## Tests

- `run::tests::the_gun_range_is_a_free_armory`:
  - every kind is owned, with one of each on the rack;
  - a bullet of every shell and set, plus every other card;
  - no upgrades, nothing to pay;
  - the same card twice, free, and still on the shelf;
  - a rack gun swaps;
  - new rolls change only the rolled bullets;
  - deeper rolls, empty the gun, empty the bag;
  - a run allows none of these.
- `start::tests::the_gun_ranges_armory_goes_back_to_the_range`:
  - the main page's row picks Range, and comes before Options;
  - Back to the range, Esc and a right click give `NextLevel`, and Leave
    gives `EndRun`;
  - Esc closes an open folder first;
  - in a run's shop, Esc still goes to the main page.
- `tests::the_gun_range_builds_a_gun_and_plays_it` (the window, on software
  Vulkan):
  - menu, then armory, then two free cards;
  - the round has no time limit, the bank, and the gun and cards;
  - H opens the armory paused over the same round;
  - a rack long gun reaches every squad, and Restart keeps it;
  - leaving keeps the gun for next time.
- `the_main_page_follows_the_ui_size` failed with the extra row: 150% on a
  1280 x 800 window only reached 1.2x. The main page now counts Quit at its
  real height (48 units, no second line), so it is held at 0.95 rather than
  0.9.

## Cost

Nothing runs every step. The armory's work happens when you open or close
it. The HUD's range lines are two string checks a frame.
