The music bed and the planet's air

Every planet now has a slow, quiet music bed in its own key, soft bell notes now and then, and its own air (wind, a river, insects, drips, a low rumble). It darkens and a low drone rises as the slime closes on the squad. Options has a new row, Music and air, to turn it down or off. Headphones help: most of it sits low.

In the game

The first 40 seconds of planets 1 to 3 (run 1, the squads bot), recorded headless with everything on. Mix meter over the full 120 s: the score is 11 to 13 dB under the whole mix, under the shots and splats, about level with the slime's own voice.
