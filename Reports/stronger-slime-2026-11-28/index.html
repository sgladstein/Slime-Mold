Stronger slime: A, B and D

Refilmed with 19 soldiers standing at the landing on free fire, never moving, so all you see is the slime coming at them. Every clip is the same game, from 1:26 to 2:02, at 3x speed. The clips start identical and split where the option first does something. The default view is the squad at zoom 6; switch to the whole map to see where the slime comes from.

Today Today's game

One stream runs down the tube into the guns, a few walkers at a time. This is the easy case you described.

A Mass, then come in wide

Once the stream reaches the ring just past your guns' reach, it stops there and piles up at two or three points round the squad for 12 s, spread out to the sides, then comes in from all of them at once for 8 s. Here it starts at 1:30, strikes at 1:42, and again from 2:35 (after this clip) with 1,600 and 1,400 walkers at two points.

The slime now arrives from two sides at once instead of one stream. Against 19 rifles it still loses (no soldier fell). If you keep it, the next lever is a longer gather so the masses are bigger when they come.

B Armoured tongue

Slime on or near your trail takes one more hit to kill.

It looks the same as today. It is a toughness number, not something new to see. In the first cut, +2 wiped a moving squad.

D Several tongues

Two branches run out from the squad toward the slime, 50 degrees either side of it. Coming in along a branch is cheaper for the slime than walking straight in, so the stream is meant to split and come in from two angles.

Not what it should be yet. The slime piles into one big body just past your range and stops feeding the guns (half the kills), instead of splitting into two tongues. It needs another pass before it is worth judging.
