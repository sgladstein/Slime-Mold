# Dead ends — tried, rejected, and the condition each rejection depends on

The mould game's entries, carried from Pixel_Physics's `Reports/dead-ends.md`
on 2026-11-10. Grep the *mechanism* you are about to touch or propose before
building it. Each entry names where it applies, what was tried, why it was
rejected and when it would be worth trying again.

- **`src/mould/mod.rs` `STORM_CUTS` -- the Mould probe's storm cutting three (or two) tubes, as the brief asked; 2026-11-07, one cut shipped** - **Three cuts split even a looped five-pile network, so the toughness star ("nothing lost") was earned in 0 of 20 runs at three cuts and 0 of 20 at two** (5 seeds x do nothing / random hand / simple plan / a plan with 10 oats spending its spares on loops). At one cut it was earned 4 of 20, never by doing nothing. A *disc*-shaped cut (radius 6) before that failed the other way: the mould flowed round it within the storm's watch, and every run scored the star. *Re-test when:* the mould heals across a scar (today, pieces that each hold a pile are each fed and never rejoin, so reading the damage at 2, 5 or 8 s gives identical joins), or the network gets denser than the dish's five piles allow. Record: `Reports/mould-probe-2026-11-07.md`.

- **`src/mould/mod.rs` `Params::fed_scent` as a player's pull dial -- the RTS brainstorm's "Taut", where each food source is set slack, even or taut so roads bow or straighten; probed by the red team on 2026-11-10, never built** - Raising one mould's eaten-pile smell from the shipped 0.15 to 1.0 at 60 s on The Wild One did not straighten roads: it pulled the mould off them. Network length at +5 s fell from 142-225 to 60-80 cells, walkers within 4 cells of a pile rose from 0.71-0.75 to 0.92-0.98, and the best piece joined 1-2 piles instead of 4 (4 of 4 paired clones, 22.5 degrees; 4 of 4 at 45). 0.5 did the same, less. Lowering it to 0 sent the mould off its food: length 323-434, then dead (0-63 walkers) by +30 s. Only 0.3 was mild, and it read as thinning, not straightening. This repeats the `deposit_food` entry above: a pile at full smell pulls every walker back onto it. *Re-test when:* the pull acts along a road (on its trail or walls) rather than through a pile's smell, or tube stops being where walkers stand. A mild pull near 0.3 may still be a small lever inside another direction. **The rejection depends on the dish (field retest, same day):** on 23 smaller mirrored piles (about 6,600 walkers) the same 1.0 pull grew the network (length 753-856 against 613-794 at +30 s) and won food share since the change, 0.653-0.710 against 0.461-0.571 (8 of 8 seeds over two layouts; 16 of 16 in the verb census, median +0.139); 0.3 also won (8 of 8); 0 still starved the mould. So it holds for few, large piles. On a many-pile dish the pull is a lever, but as one dial for the whole mould it is always best at full: a stat, not a choice, until it is set per pile or costs something.
