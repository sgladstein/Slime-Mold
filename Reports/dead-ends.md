# Dead ends — tried, rejected, and the condition each rejection depends on

The mould game's entries, carried from Pixel_Physics's `Reports/dead-ends.md`
on 2026-11-10. Grep the *mechanism* you are about to touch or propose before
building it. Each entry names where it applies, what was tried, why it was
rejected and when it would be worth trying again.

- **`src/mould/mod.rs` `STORM_CUTS` -- the Mould probe's storm cutting three (or two) tubes, as the brief asked; 2026-11-07, one cut shipped** - **Three cuts split even a looped five-pile network, so the toughness star ("nothing lost") was earned in 0 of 20 runs at three cuts and 0 of 20 at two** (5 seeds x do nothing / random hand / simple plan / a plan with 10 oats spending its spares on loops). At one cut it was earned 4 of 20, never by doing nothing. A *disc*-shaped cut (radius 6) before that failed the other way: the mould flowed round it within the storm's watch, and every run scored the star. *Re-test when:* the mould heals across a scar (today, pieces that each hold a pile are each fed and never rejoin, so reading the damage at 2, 5 or 8 s gives identical joins), or the network gets denser than the dish's five piles allow. Record: `Reports/mould-probe-2026-11-07.md`.
- **`src/mould/mod.rs` `deposit_food` -- food scent laid into the Jones trail field itself (one channel, as in Jones 2010), and a pile keeping its full smell once eaten; 2026-11-07, both replaced** - One channel drew and counted the halo round every pile as tube, so piles wore pale rings and the tube-connectivity score joined nothing. A pile at full smell after the mould reached it pulled every walker back onto it: do nothing joined 1 of 5 and never explored. Scent is now its own field summed into the sensors, and an eaten pile keeps 15% of its smell (`Params::fed_scent`). *Re-test when:* tube is defined by something other than a trail threshold. Record: `Reports/mould-probe-2026-11-07.md`.
- **`src/mould/mod.rs` `Params::light_weight` -- the Mould probe's lamp as a repulsive term in every sensor (with a per-cell trail fade and a burn), the Jones-model way to steer by light; 2026-11-08, replaced by `push_light`** - The playtest review's top complaint: the lamp erased the tube it was meant to steer. Held on a tube for 3 s against a dark twin, the sensor lamp cut the food join on 5 of 5 seeds at the shipped settings (weight 400, fade 0.25, burn), and every lighter setting tried (weight 20, 60 or 400 with fade and burn off; reach 16 or 24) still lost the join while lit: walkers that only *turn* away leave the lit stretch empty, and an empty stretch is a cut. The push lamp carries trail and walkers outward a cell at a time, so the tube parts round the light and stays joined (5 of 5 held on it; beside it, shifted 5-7 cells, joined 5 of 5 against the old lamp's 3 of 5). *Re-test when:* tube connectivity stops being a trail threshold, or the lamp becomes a tool meant to cut. Record: `Reports/mould-probe-2026-11-07.md`, *Playtest fixes*.
- **`src/mould/mod.rs` `Params::sensor_offset` 9 -> 6, 7 or 8 -- finer tubes by shortening how far ahead each walker feels (Jones's sensor offset), as the 2026-11-10 review suggested; measured on all five dishes 2026-11-10, kept at 9** - The tubes do get a little thinner and branch more, but the mould senses food and oats from less far, so every planned solution gets worse. Stars per seed for the scripted hand, 8 seeds, reach 9/8/7/6: Behind the Wall 33333333 / 33333333 / 13333323 / 20031231; Beat the Ruler 33333333 / 30330300 / 00000000 / 00000000 (the third pile never joined in time); Running Dry 3s throughout bar one seed at 6. At reach 6 Keep It Alive's lamp+oats+water hand lived shorter on 6 of 8 seeds (median 6:21 -> 3:41) and The Wild One's all-tools share fell from 0.67-1.00 to 0.49-0.63. Doing nothing stayed at 0 stars everywhere. *Re-test when:* the dishes are retuned for a shorter reach (oats closer, piles nearer), or the thin look is wanted on a new dish built for it; the thin look itself belongs in the drawing. Do not re-test by trying 8.5: Beat the Ruler already loses half its seeds at 8. Record: `Reports/mould-wild-one-2026-11-10.md`, *Finer tubes*; page https://claude.ai/artifact/5JT3D1sv7CwSRmYfkR6VTW.
- **`src/mould/level.rs` The Wild One's late piles -- three piles landing on the seam between the moulds (0:45, 1:20, 1:50; 30k, 30k, 25k food) so the middle of the game has a race, with the scripted warden's oats chasing each; 2026-11-10, taken out** - Review 2 found letting go at 0:30 kept the same star as playing on.
