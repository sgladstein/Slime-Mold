What to look for

  • Crimson slime on a soldier is slime biting it, and it shows only while the soldier is bitten. Gold slime, even right beside a soldier, is not biting.
  • A soldier flushing red is taking damage, a flash for every 3 health it loses, so one deep in the slime flushes again and again.
  • The squad's helmets stay white, so you can still count it while it is being bitten.

Why it was hidden

A soldier is bitten by every walker within its touch, 1.5 cells, and its figure is drawn 1.15 times wider, so it covered all of them. What you saw was the slime round it, which does no damage. Crimson is the bite's own colour now, and it is drawn on top of the soldier. Every other light of the fight was checked too: a hit's, a burst's and a flare's light are the size of what they do.

Filmed on the software renderer, 30 frames a game second. A crimson pixel has red of at least 120, green under 0.35 of the red, and blue above green; the slime's gold and orange and the blood's dark red all have blue below green. Source and numbers: Reports/biting-slime-2026-11-18/ in the repo.
