Planet 3, the Trunk: cords with an edge

The Trunk keeps its trail longest of any slime, so off its body the trail fanned out as wide blurred plumes and every cord wore a glowing halo about 20 px wide at zoom 6. The polish review called it "a flat translucent yellow sheet". Now a cord is its trail only where the trail stands thick enough, so the cord's rim is the trail's own contour, about a cell wide, and still rises and takes the light. The faint tail past it shows on the fen's mud as the slick track the trail already leaves there. Before is on the left and after on the right. It is the same game on both sides, and only the view shader differs.

What changed

  • A cord is the trail times a smooth step from 0.15 to 0.3 (CORD_EDGE), the same step the Drifter's sheet took this morning. Its colour (CORD_VEIL) and its height (CORD_RISE) both take it.
  • Only the Trunk (planet 3's slime look) changes. Cost: one smooth step a fine cell in prep.
  • It bends the "no thresholded edge" ruling a little: the edge is a smooth step about a cell wide, on the simulation's own trail.

Not done

  • The review also asked for a darker core. The lit rim and the cord's own rise read as a tube already, so I left the core as it is.
