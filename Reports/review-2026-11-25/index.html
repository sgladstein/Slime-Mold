Findings, most important first

1

The slime can't reach a squad that moves, and the fix so far is a stack of timers

The 11-24 balance review says it directly: "the slime can't reach a squad he steers." You win with 10 to 16 soldiers on planets 1 and 2 and lose 0 to 3 a planet. The 11-15 whole-game review found the same thing ("the slime sits at home"). Your notes swing between the two ends: too easy on 11-18, too hard on 11-20, "so much easier" on 11-21, too easy again on 11-22.

Each swing was met by adding or retuning a scheduled threat, gated by planet. There are now raids, armoured marchers, strikers, waves, the late lunge, the last stand, mini-bosses (three at once from planet 6), the Pinwheel, the slime's mortar, fruiting, starfall, twists, and the heart's armour by planet. Each one works, but most are events dropped onto the simulation, not something growing out of it. That pulls against two of your standing rules: the mould is integral to play, and the picture is the simulation.

Filmed on today's main (planet 1, greedy hand). At 3:00 the slime sits whole inside its amber ring at home while 44 soldiers drain the map's crystals one after another.
2

Every tool is optional so far

Last night's run bot used a plain rifle and bought no gun cards, pumps, turrets, walls or lures. It won planets 1, 2 and 3 in 5:53, 3:54 and 4:35 with about 30 soldiers, all its income from draining by hand (pumped=0). The curve passes also play cards=0. The old demo bar was "several strategies that counter each other." Main hasn't shown that yet. Pumps were meant to be the main income (#322), and the bot never builds one. The 11-24 counters films show a good counter for each slime in a one-minute clip. The rifle still wins the whole planet without them.

Run bot, run 45661564052, seed 2: pumps=0 turrets=0 walls=0 lures=0 gun=none, won 3 of 3.
3

Two hours needs more than longer planets

Planets take 4 to 9 minutes (bot medians), and your all-planet win on 11-22 took about 77 minutes. Making planets 1.3 to 1.8 times longer adds playing time, but it stretches fights that are already decided by the middle. More planets need more variety per planet. Nothing between planets lets you choose where to go: the run is a fixed line of eight with a shop between each. A route choice is the usual roguelike way to add length and replay value together. It would also give the twists, rescue goals and slime species a place to sit as choices.

6

The project is getting heavy

The game is 151,000 lines of Rust plus 11,500 of shader code, with 107 rule fields in rules.rs. The old CPU slime engine (big.rs, 7,700 lines) is still in, and D8 hasn't been asked in eight days. Lane handoffs, meant as "state, branch, next step," now run 400 to 780 lines. The in-game "What is new" list gained 310 lines in nine days, too many to read. "Things to try" (five lines) is the one that works.

The test is a 15-soldier squad that kites on planet 2 with raids and strikers off: does it lose soldiers it can't avoid by moving? If yes, some timers can go.
