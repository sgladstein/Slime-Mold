Crystal Hearts

The slime's heart is a mini-boss now, not the way to win. Wherever the slime keeps a crystal packed thick for two minutes, a heart grows there and fights for it. You win a planet by wiping out every last bit of slime.

The rule

Each second, every crystal that still has charge is checked: if its slime covers 85% or more of the crystal, that crystal's clock runs. If the slime is thinned below that (fire that holes it), the clock falls back three seconds for every second. At two minutes a heart forms on that crystal, with the same size, armour, lunges and call-home that the old heart had on each planet. When you break it, what is left of it turns back into ordinary slime and the crystal's clock restarts from zero. Breaking it does nothing else. One heart lives at a time, and another crystal with a full clock grows its heart as soon as the first one breaks.
