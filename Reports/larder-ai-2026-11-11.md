# Larder player AI: a harness that plays through the player's commands (2026-11-11)

Asked for by the owner at 14:07Z ("a player AI thread, for automated testing so you can keep developing without me having to playtest everything", and later "two soldier teams against multiple molds so in the future the AI could also play soldiers"). Code: `src/mould/larder_ai.rs` (the AI), `examples/larderai.rs` (census, look, replays), `src/bin/larder.rs` (`ai=STYLE` to watch it play). Raw output in [larder-ai-2026-11-11/](larder-ai-2026-11-11/).

**This is a harness to port, not a balance result.** The owner ruled no more small-dish tuning (Larder thread, 14:12Z); Larder is moving to a GPU world ([roadmap](larder-world-roadmap-2026-11-11.md)). The AI was built on today's CPU field dish so it could be built and checked now. The numbers below are about the AI. They tell you nothing about whether the game is balanced.

## What it is

- **It plays through the player's hands.** The AI only gives `Cmd`s through `Game::apply`, the same orders, stances, buys, turrets and bait the window's mouse and keys give. So every AI round is a replay file, and `larder replay FILE` plays it back to the same dish hash (test `an_ai_round_is_a_replay`). It never calls `Game::shoot`. The M1-M3 scripted plans call `shoot`, `order` and the game's RNG directly, so they are not replays.
- **It sees what a player sees.** It reads a `View`: its own soldiers and turrets, the bank, the piles (food, and whether mould is on each), the mould as walker counts on an 8-cell grid, a drop once its smell is out, and the clock. It does not read scent, headings or the hunger number.
- **It commands a `Side`**: a home and the soldiers it owns. Today a side is the whole squad. A second soldier team later is a second side with its own brain (test `a_side_commands_only_its_own`). The Larder thread agreed (14:12Z) to add a team id to `Cmd` when the world version gets a second side.
- **It is one brain turned by knobs** (`Style`), with five presets a player would name. The knobs can be changed from the command line (`lure:buy_to=4+bait=0`), so an exploiter search needs no new code.
  - **turtle**: a ring burning round home from the start, turrets, farms the larder only (M1's fortress).
  - **boom**: farms the ten nearest piles and buys a soldier for each, no bait, hunts with 24 (M3's farmer, plus a hunt).
  - **rush**: buys all it can arm and attacks from the first second (M2's hunter).
  - **lure**: baits the mould away and farms three piles, then buys and hunts at 6:00 whatever it sees (M3's striker).
  - **adapt**: M2's reader. It baits and farms three piles while the mould is big. Once the mould is small it farms eight with a soldier each, and it hunts with sixteen.
- **Controls** are idle (no commands) and the random hand: once a game second, with chance 0.5, it gives one random command (an order to a random spot or pile, a stance, a buy, a turret or bait near a soldier).
- **`set=name=value`** changes any `Rules` field by name (`larder_ai::set_rule`) for balance tests.

## Two AI mistakes the first census found, fixed

- **Buying soldiers it could not arm.** At 6:00, lure bought 28 soldiers and emptied the bank. None of them could fire, and 28 died in 25 s, each one a meal for the mould (seed 1, traced with `trace=5`). Now a buy has to leave `ammo` (30) game seconds of fire for the whole squad in the bank. Before the fix, lure was wiped on 8 of 8 fed seeds (1-8). After it, lure held to the cap on 6 of 8 and won 8 of 8 lean.
- **Bait laid every second while hunting.** The hunt fights the mould near home, so bait was eaten the moment it landed and a new 300 was laid each think. Bait is now only laid while farming. The hunt also keeps a target while mould is still near it, not only while mould is on it; before that, the squad walked between three targets in 9 s.
- **Rejected for boom:** two guards burning round home whenever 60 walkers came within 30 cells. On seeds 1-8 the squad was wiped 8 of 8 in both fields (median end 145 s). Without the guards it held to the cap 8 of 8 fed and won 8 of 8 lean. Turtle keeps its ring, because the ring is what turtle is.
- **Before the bait-only-when-big fix**, adapt won 2 of 8 lean rounds (seeds 1-8). The reader baited only while the mould was big. With that change, adapt won 5 of 8.

## Census

Deciding measures, named before the held-out run: (1) each working style beats idle and the random hand on 15+ of 16 paired held-out seeds in both fields, surviving Holm over all 20 tests in that field; (2) the best style per seed is split, not one style on 13+ of 16; (3) the AI's think costs well under a step. Seeds 1-16 were used while building; 17-32 were not looked at until the end. Scores are `End::score`: a win is in (1, 3], a stalemate at the cap in [-0.5, 0.5], a loss in [-2, -1).

Held out, seeds 17-32 ([census-17-32.txt](larder-ai-2026-11-11/census-17-32.txt)):

| Hand | Fed median (win/cap/loss) | Lean median (win/cap/loss) | Best on, fed / lean |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| idle | -1.787 (0/0/16) | -1.791 (0/0/16) | - |
| random | -1.890 (0/0/16) | -1.881 (0/0/16) | - |
| turtle | -1.913 (0/0/16) | -1.912 (0/0/16) | 0 / 0 |
| rush | -1.914 (0/0/16) | -1.914 (0/0/16) | 0 / 0 |
| boom | -0.389 (0/15/1) | 2.035 (15/0/1) | 5 / 12 |
| lure | -0.500 (0/8/8) | 1.934 (16/0/0) | 6 / 4 |
| adapt | -0.400 (0/16/0) | 0.500 (7/9/0) | 5 / 0 |

- **Measure 1 passes for boom, lure and adapt.** Each beats idle and random on 16 of 16 in both fields (p 0.00003, survives Holm over 20). Turtle and rush lose to idle on 16 of 16. This matches M3, where the scripted fortress and hunter also lost to idle: in today's game those plans lose, and the AI playing them is not the reason.
- **Measure 2:** fed, the best style is split 5/6/5 (boom/lure/adapt). Lean, boom is best on 12 and lure on 4. The only paired differences that survive Holm among the working styles are lean: boom over adapt 15 of 16 and lure over adapt 16 of 16. Fed, boom against lure is 9 of 15 and boom against adapt 11 of 16, so neither survives.
- **Against the scripted plans** (M3 report, same seeds; different code, so not paired): the scripted reader's medians were -0.250 fed and 1.586 lean. Adapt, its port through commands, gets -0.400 and 0.500, so it is weaker lean (7 wins against the reader's 16). Boom and lure, playing through commands, win more lean rounds than any M3 script did.
- **Measure 3, cost:** one think takes 15-39 µs, once a game second (every 15 steps), so 1-3 µs a step on average. The game layer's own step is 30 µs (M3).

Seeds 1-16 ([census-1-16.txt](larder-ai-2026-11-11/census-1-16.txt)) agree: boom, lure and adapt beat idle and random 16 of 16. The best style is split 5/10/1 fed and 12/4/0 lean.

## How to use it

```
cargo run --release --example larderai -- census seeds=17-32 [lean=1] [hands=boom,lure:strike_at=300] [set=price=20]
cargo run --release --example larderai -- look=DIR seeds=17 hand=boom trace=10
cargo run --release --example larderai -- replays=DIR seeds=17-20
cargo run --release --bin larder -- 17 ai=lure      # watch it play; your commands still go in
```
