Bomb rounds

A new payload card, bomb, for the gun rounds. It kills a small core, then throws the live mould in a ring around it straight outward. The thrown walkers fly for up to half a second, landing where they stop, and they live on there. So a bomb cuts the body and also spreads it. Nothing here is painted on: the sparks are the thrown walkers themselves, the bright band is the trail they lay as they fly, and the hole is the kill.

Shape C on the Fork, seed 1, software Vulkan. Every film plays at real time, 15 frames a game second. The impact light from the gun rounds work is not in these films yet.

How it works

  • A bomb kills everything within 4 dish cells, leaves a 6-second crater there, and lays alarm out to 8 cells. Every living walker on the ground out to 10 cells (2.5 times the kill) is thrown.
  • A thrown walker flies straight out from the centre at 1.5 times a walker's step, for up to 0.5 s. The ones nearest the kill fly furthest, about 11 dish cells, and the ones at the ring's edge barely move.
  • It lays 3 times a walker's trail as it flies, so the throw draws a bright band, and the view draws it as a hot speck while it is in the air. It stops at a wall and lands on the first free cell. It can eat, breed or doze as soon as it lands.
  • Cards: bomb on its own, cluster (three small bombs round the hit) and blowout (1.6 times wider). They are in rounds= and the H cycle. A bomb costs 60 food, as a blast does.
  • It runs on both engines. The engine's throw matches the CPU's exactly on software Vulkan, every walker at every step of the flight. A twin with a wrong speed planted in it fails. The GTX 1070 twin is pending until the PC is free.
