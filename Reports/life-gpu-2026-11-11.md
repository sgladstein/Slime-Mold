# The tiers' life cycle on the GPU (2026-11-11)

The tiers thread built three Larder ladder tiers on the CPU big dish, each
with a counter ([mould tiers](mould-tiers-2026-11-11.md)): the **Sporer**
(fed walkers fly off as spores; struck, it scatters), the **Sleeper** (a
dormant crust that wakes in a wave on scent or alarm) and the **Swarmer**
(walkers drawn to the alarm a strike lays), and the player's strike
(`BigDish::strike`). The owner asked (11-11 20:12Z, through the tiers
thread) for the tiers in the game; the game runs on the GPU engine. This
report puts all of it on `gpu::Engine`, so the sandbox and the Larder world
both get it, and checks it against the CPU. The `BigParams` doc comments
are the spec (the tiers thread's word); nothing in the CPU rules changed.
The tiers thread's PR #85 (merged 11-12) added two rules on the way, and
they are here too: a dormant walker dies asleep with chance `sleep_death`
a tick (the Sleeper's 1e-4, so an unfed crust fades), and a squad shot
that hits lays `squad::SHOT_ALARM` (50) on the agar within twice its
radius. `mould::tiers::Tier::settings` now sets every tier on the GPU.

Every rule is off by default, so no dish changes. The gate: the GPU matches
the CPU tick for tick on dishes that use them, planted bugs fail that test,
and the cost is quoted.

```
MOULDGPU_TEST_GPU=1 cargo test --release --locked --bin mouldgpu -- --nocapture the_life_cycle squad_matches a_wall_builds an_alarm_without
cargo run --release --bin mouldgpu -- tier=sleeper
```

## The controls

All are `set` keys of the sandbox and the engine (`gpu::set`) under the
CPU's names, and `BigParams` passes them through:

- Spores: `spore_rate`, `spore_scent`, `spore_alarm`, `spore_burst`,
  `spore_steps`, `spore_speed`, `spore_reach`, `spore_deposit`,
  `spore_land`, `spore_rest`.
- Dormancy: `sleep_rate`, `sleep_deposit`, `wake_scent`, `wake_alarm`,
  `wake_steps`, `sleep_rest`, `sleep_death`.
- The alarm: `alarm_keep` (the field exists when it is over 0),
  `alarm_weight` (walkers sense it, + toward, - away), `alarm_relay` (what a
  waking walker adds).
- The strike: `Engine::strike(shared, s, x, y, r, alarm, alarm_r)` in dish
  cells, as `BigDish::strike`; `struck()` reads back the walkers killed,
  `life_of()` every slot's state and timer, `alarm_of()` the field.
- The squad's shots (`Engine::set_squad`) lay their alarm by themselves
  when the field is on; nothing to set.
- A tier: `tier=sporer`, `tier=sleeper` or `tier=swarmer` on the sandbox's
  command line (a `set` key), `Tier::Sleeper.settings(&mut s)` in code, or
  `Tier::apply` on the CPU's `BigParams`, sets its keys.

The headless run prints a `life:` settings line and, at the end, the
walkers, spores, dormant and dead.

## How it runs

- **State in the spec word.** Bits 9-10 hold the state (0 walker, 1 spore,
  2 dormant, 3 dead), bits 16-31 the timer, beside the species (0-7) and
  kind (8). Two marks carry a decision across passes: FRUIT (bit 11) and
  DOZE (bit 12). A child takes its parent's species and kind only, as a
  walker with timer 0.
- **Passes, in the CPU's order.** `sense` decides, from the fields as the
  tick began, whether an ordinary walker with its timer out fruits or falls
  dormant (the mark), and senses the alarm with `alarm_weight`. `walk`
  moves only ordinary unmarked walkers and counts the dead's and the marked
  walkers' counters down. Then, after any wall bites, three new passes:
  `fate` (timers; a marked walker leaves its cell as a spore or lies down;
  the dormant die with chance `sleep_death`, else lay and wake on scent, or
  on alarm with their timer out, and a waking walker relays), `fly` (spores fly straight the first half and home
  on scent the second, lay a streak and claim a landing) and `alight` (the
  lowest claim lands, rests `spore_rest`). The strike is two passes run on
  their own between ticks: `hit` (cells: trail and each species' trail
  cleared within `r`, alarm added on agar within `alarm_r`) and `kill`
  (walkers on the ground within `r`; spores in flight are missed).
- **Dying asleep** (`fate`): the keyed draw `DRAW_SLEEP_DIE` (118), as the
  CPU's. The walker leaves its cell (and clears the cell's claim, as a
  starving one does, so a birth can take it this tick), is dead, and with
  growth frees its slot; it lays nothing and is not counted dormant. Its
  sick counter counts down and, without growth, it ages, as every slot does
  on the CPU.
- **A shot's alarm** (`shout`, after the squad's `cut`, with the field on):
  one workgroup takes the shots in order, a thread a cell of the shot's box
  (29 x 29 at the widest, radius 14), with a barrier after each shot so two
  discs on one cell never race. `squad::pack` sends each shot's alarm
  radius beside its strike's (both squared and floored, so the two sides
  test the same integers), and the squad's row in `Params` carries
  `SHOT_ALARM`.
- **The alarm without the states.** The field is on with `alarm_keep` over
  0 whether or not any walker has a state, as the CPU makes it with the
  dish. The first version also needed the states, which a strike or spores
  or dormancy switch on: on a Swarmer dish (the alarm and nothing else) a
  shot's alarm would have been laid and never spread or sensed. The field
  now switches the outside pipelines on by itself.
- **Only ordinary walkers** eat, pay upkeep, starve, breed, jump and count
  in the capped deposit's classes; the dormant hold their cell (they count
  in `occ` and in the capped share) but take nothing from a crystal. Every
  living slot is converted, ages and counts its sick counter down, as on
  the CPU.
- **No new binding.** The alarm is a second half of the germ field; the
  per-cell life counts (relays, the dormant now) are two more thirds of the
  germ counts; the spore and sleep lays are two more halves of the blocked
  counts. `Params` grows from 688 to 832 bytes: a life block after the
  species block, the strike's row last (`STRIKE_AT` 800). `sleep_death`
  and its draw take two of the life block's spare words, and `SHOT_ALARM`
  the squad row's spare one.
- **Switches.** The life passes run once spores or dormancy are on, or a
  strike has been made (the CPU makes its states then too), and stay on;
  they switch the outside pipelines on. The alarm field is cleared when it
  first comes on.

## A landing bug found on the way

The first version claimed a landing cell only for a spore whose species
has a crowd limit. On the CPU every lander claims: a spore of a species
without a limit lands either way, but its claim can beat a limited
species' spore to the cell. Where species with their own limits met, the
GPU let both land. Round 1's misses called for a dish where a burst lands
all at once; with two species, one of them unlimited, it showed 13 walkers,
8 timers and 21 cell counts off over four ticks. Every lander claims now,
and the lowest claim clears the cell (`fly`, `alight`); that dish matches
exactly.

## Cost

Software Vulkan (llvmpipe), so CPU numbers, not a GPU's: the sandbox's
default dish (100,000 walkers, one a cell, food), a frame of two ticks,
headless, 5 s a run, three rounds
([`cost.sh`](life-gpu-2026-11-11/cost.sh),
[`raw/cost.txt`](life-gpu-2026-11-11/raw/cost.txt)). BASE is main before
the port (7e8dacc; the GPU code is the same on main after #85). GPU ms of
ticks a frame:

| Pair | BASE | NEW | NEW - BASE |
|---|---|---|---|
| plain (the outside rules compiled out) | 28.8, 26.7, 28.9 | 30.0, 28.3, 30.4 | +1.2, +1.6, +1.5 |
| plain, NEW run first ([`raw/cost-plain-swapped.txt`](life-gpu-2026-11-11/raw/cost-plain-swapped.txt)) | 29.3, 29.8, 29.8 | 29.5, 25.9, 28.3 | +0.2, -3.9, -1.5 |
| forced (outside on, every rule off) | 39.6, 40.5, 39.3 | 40.1, 39.5, 40.0 | +0.5, -0.9, +0.7 |

Off, the port costs nothing measurable: the plain pair's first three were
all up, but with the order swapped two of three were down (4 of 6 up, sign
test p = 0.69), so the first three were the order, not the code. With the
rules on, the dish changes (spores fly, the dormant stop moving), so the
passes say more than the frame: with spores, dormancy and the alarm on,
`fate` takes 2.2 ms a tick, `fly` 0.26 and `alight` 0.15, while `sense`
falls from 4.1 to 1.8 and `walk` from 1.7 to 0.7 with most walkers
dormant. Dying asleep adds nothing visible (`fate` 1.6 ms then, with fewer
left to decide). The tiers, each alone on the dish, take 40-42 ms
(Sporer), 40-44 (Sleeper) and 38-39 (Swarmer) against forced's 40: the
alarm field alone costs no more than switching the outside pipelines on.
After 5 s the Sleeper has 83,559 of 100,000 walkers dormant and 4,348
dead; the Sporer 38 spores in flight.

The shot alarm runs once a frame with shots, in one workgroup: at most 4
cells a thread a shot (841 cells, 256 threads); the squad test's frame
does not separate it from the shots' other passes. The GTX 1070's timing
waits for the owner's PC (the Remote Control session there is parked).
