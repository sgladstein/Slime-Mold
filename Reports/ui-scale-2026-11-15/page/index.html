UI size, the one-row bar and the hover card

Three asks from the owner on 11-15. 07:51Z: "everything was supposed to be on one row, why is the gun in a seperate row on top". 07:54Z: the card over the mould's home crystal gets in the way of shooting. 07:56Z: "Changing the UI /text percentage ... the menus are unaffected. The gun range is unaffected (I cannot read any of the text)". Frames from the cloud's software GPU; "before" is main before these changes.

What was wrong

  • MenusEach page was held to whatever fitted the window's height. On a 1080-line screen the options and key pages drew at 0.85 whatever the UI size, and the shop and the gun range's armory shrank to fit, the armory to about 0.6. So Ctrl + did almost nothing there. Now every page draws at the UI size, and a page taller than the window scrolls with the wheel or the arrow keys (the keyboard's row is kept in view). A thin bar on the right shows where you are. At big sizes the shop puts the armory under the upgrades instead of squeezing it into one column.
  • BarThe gun strip moved above the bar whenever the squad panel would be left under 240 units. The wider strip from the morning's review made that happen at big UI sizes (2560×1440 at 200%). It now always stays in the row and draws smaller, down to 60%. The squad panel's buttons (Let go, Combine, Split, Guards) no longer run over "1 squad" or onto the gun strip: they get smaller, then drop the header, then their key letters, then the last button.
  • HoverThe card over a crystal or the larder waits for the cursor to rest 0.35 s, shows 2 s, fades, and stays away until the cursor is on something else. Any click puts it away. Before, it stayed up the whole time the cursor was on the thing, which is where you shoot. (No picture: the card follows real time, which the picture tool does not.)
  • F1The key card in play had one column. With squad mode's forty-odd rows it ran off a 1080-line screen at big sizes. It now uses up to three columns, and shrinks only past that.
