# One body: cohesion, hunger and cuts (2026-11-12)

The owner's playtest of `larder-world` (2026-11-12 04:13Z): "the slime didn't
feel like one cohesive organism". On a cut-off part (04:17-04:18Z): "It can
separate and it will starve eventually after being cut off, but it also can
act as it's own smaller organism if cut of and attack or find it's own food
source or try to rejoin the main slime before starving", and "it should still
feel like a cut off hunk and not a bunch of individuals".

Everything here is on the CPU race world the game uses
(`larder_world::race_params`, scale 2, 5,000 walkers at the start, 15 ticks a
game second), with `examples/cutprobe.rs`. All new rules are off by default;
the default dish hash (`bigdish preset=dense seconds=2 blob=0 piles=1`) is
still 869141a7bf398adf. Page: https://claude.ai/artifact/E9MSpcgBj7F8jZX6i6LA5q
(copy in `one-body-2026-11-12/page.html`; the blind A/B key is
[`one-body-2026-11-12/ab-key.md`](one-body-2026-11-12/ab-key.md)). Raw output: `one-body-2026-11-12/raw/`.

## What today's mould is

Grown 60-300 game s, the mould is dense lumps on crystals joined by
single-file streams of walkers (pictures on the page). The streams are what
read as "a bunch of individuals". At 20,000 starting walkers (`walkers=20000`,
seed 1, 180 s) it reads much more like one body (raw `dens/`), so part of the
look is density: the game runs 5,000 where the GPU is built for 100,000.

## Rules added (`BigParams`, all off by default, CPU only)

- `hunger_scent`, `hunger_at`: a walker's scent reading times 1 +
  `hunger_scent` x (1 - energy / `hunger_at`), floored at 1.
- `body_pull`, `body_r`, `body_lone`: sensors also read walkers in a square
  round their point, weighted by the walker's own loneliness.
- `tether`, `tether_hold`: a walker may step only to a cell touching at
  least `tether` others (or as many as it touches now); with `tether_hold`
  a keyed share holds still each step and movers count only holders.
- `lone_upkeep`, `lone_r`: upkeep times this for a walker with no other
  walker within `lone_r` fine cells.
- The existing `energy_flow` (food evens out between touching cells).

## Cuts

Cuts across the network (`cutprobe` default: a band across the path back
from a far tip) mostly cut nothing off: the network loops, and the "pieces"
were stretches of one stream (raw `cut4/`). Ring cuts instead
(`ring=6 cutw=4`): every walker on a ring 6-10 dish cells from a centre
killed and its trail cleared, the hunk inside followed.

- **Gaps close in 1-2 s.** Walkers move 30 fine cells a game second (2
  substeps x 15 ticks), about 15 dish cells, faster than soldiers (10). With
  the gap open the main body touched the hunk again within 2 s in every ring
  cut on 4 seeds under today's rules, H, and the lone-upkeep sets (raw
  `cut6/`, `cut10/`), and at half step (`step` 0.5) as well.
- **A hunk held apart stays one lump.** With the ring held off the agar for
  30 s (probe only), hunks on crystals sat as round lumps (largest part 1.00,
  compactness 0.99 at 20 s; raw `cut7/`); hunks off food stayed one lump and
  starved (to a tenth of its walkers): today in 17-23 s, with the `lone_upkeep` sets in 9-26 s (raw
  `cut8/`). On the page's seed 2, H's 398-walker hunk dies together by 10 s
  and today's 229-walker hunk by 23 s.
- **Hunks do not hunt yet.** Of 18 hunks cut off food with the ring held,
  one found a crystal (raw `cut8/`). With only the home side held
  (`half=1`), the main body went round the ends within 2 s (raw `cut9/`).

## Hunks left alone (05:40-06:10Z, after the owner went to bed)

The coordinator's note (05:35Z, the owner asleep): go ahead with a hungry
hunk that creeps toward food. `alone=1` removes the rest of the mould at
the cut, so the hunk has the world to itself. New keys: `hunger_reach`
(a starving walker smells further) and `hunt_at` (the hunger at which a
walker hunts; with it, `tether` binds only hunting walkers). Raw
`raw/hunt/`.

- **Food is never far.** On the race world every unfed hunk (12 a rule set,
  4 seeds) ate crystal within 2 game s, whatever the rules: crystals sit
  within the 15 dish cells a hunk crosses in a second.
- **A hunk alone scatters.** It runs out along the old roads in single
  file: largest part at 20 s, mean of 12, H 0.45; H + `tether` 1
  (`tether_hold` 0.25 or 0.5) 0.58-0.59; H + `tether` 2 0.58; adding
  `blocked_deposit` 0.5 or `body_pull` 3-10 did not help (0.50-0.58). None
  reaches "one hunk".
- **`hunger_reach` for every walker breaks the fed mould** (3: walkers smell
  past their own crystal, 0-1,900 walkers at 150 s against H's
  6,600-11,900), hence `hunt_at`; at `hunt_at` 0.8 it does not engage,
  because the hunk eats within 2 s and never starves.

So the hunk question needs a world where food is not within a second's walk
of every piece (larger maps, the designed maps, or a slower mould) before
hunting can be told from luck.

## Hunks alone on the designed maps (06:40-07:00Z; raw `raw/maps/`)

To test whether food on the race world was simply too close, I ran the same
alone-hunk probe on three designed maps (`cutprobe map=`). The rings were 8
dish cells, with no crystal within 8 dish cells of the hunk, on 6 seeds,
grown 120 s. Arms: today, H, and Hr (H plus `hunger_reach` 3, `hunt_at` 0.3).

(On flats, today and H gave no hunk that met the rule.)

- **Every hunk finds food, in about 2 s, on every map.** Even 8 dish cells
  from food, a hunk crosses that gap in under a second. Finding food is not
  what's missing.
- **What's missing is holding together on the way.** The largest part at
  20 s is 0.27-0.58 in every arm. The hunk reaches the crystal as a stream,
  not as one lump.
- **Side findings on the whole mould at 120 s.** On rim and flats, H is one
  part at 2-2.5x today's size: on flats 10k walkers against 4k, and 12-13
  crystals eaten from against 5-6. On fork, H gives no clear gain (largest
  part 0.39-0.79 against today's 0.37-0.71). Hr keeps the mould compact
  (rms 15-17 dish cells on fork) but it barely spreads (1 crystal of 10), so
  it is no default.

So the next rule to try is cohesion while moving, not smell.

**Align, tried and reverted (07:10-07:30Z, raw `raw/maps/*-align.txt`).**
A walker turned `align` of the way toward the mean heading of the walkers
within 2 cells. At 0.3 or 0.6, and also gated to hungry walkers, the hunks
were not reliably tighter than with H alone. Their largest part at 20 s
went 0.39 to 0.52-0.53 on fork (paired 7-2, p 0.18) and 0.50 to 0.42-0.55
on rim (9-8, 11-6). Meanwhile the whole mould split more: rim's largest
part fell from 0.98 to 0.70-0.85. Walkers in a tube already head along it,
so aligning them makes streams longer, not rounder. It is logged in
`Reports/dead-ends.md`. The next candidate is a pull toward a piece's
centre of mass, which needs a piece id (which the sound and graphics
threads also want).

  today's size, whatever the sharing rate. Hungry walkers find more food.

  random's Attack orders starve the whole mould on 9 of 16 seeds. Energy

2. **A new rule shape** that makes the body hold together without feeding

## On the GPU

`energy_flow`, `hunger_scent` and `hunger_at` run on the engine too (keys of
the same names; `race_settings` carries them). Two passes after eating and
before `life`: `esum` adds each ordinary walker's energy (fixed point,
x 65,536, so the sum does not depend on the order) and a count to its cell
in one buffer (binding 46); `egain` moves each walker's energy as the CPU's
`flow_energy` does, and `life` clears the sums. Hunger multiplies the scent
in `sense_at`. `Params` gains one row at 848 (`BODY_AT`, asserted).

Twin test (`the_world_matches_the_cpu_for_one_tick`, dish "one body"):
energy L1 1.4e-6 against the CPU, 0 walkers off of 15,000. Planted bugs it
catches: no gain (energy L1 3.6e-2), hunger ignored (469 walkers off), sums
not cleared (6,805 cells left; the test reads the buffer back).

Cost on the software GPU (llvmpipe, 50k slots, race-world crystals at 10 s,
median of 11 ticks each pass alone): `esum` 0.73 ms + `egain` 0.56 ms a tick,
on a tick of about 10 ms (+13%). `esum` is two atomics a walker, which
llvmpipe makes slow. The GTX 1070 figure is still to take.
