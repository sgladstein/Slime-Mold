Each cell is the median score, with the ends below it: mould wiped / time cap / squad wiped. A win scores above 1; a stalemate at the cap scores about −0.4 to +0.5. The number after each mould is how many paired seeds it has: the run was stopped early, so the Sleeper has 11, the Grudge 14 with no toolkit rows, and the Swarmer did not run. A shaded cell beats the runner-up after a Holm correction over all 125 tests; every other column is a tie. The last six columns come from the toolkit harness (kitplans) on the same world and seeds.
