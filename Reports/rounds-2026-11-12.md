# Rounds in Larder World: first build and census

2026-11-12. The owner asked for the rounds system to be built ("Build it",
16:08Z) after the [Flashes of Chaos study](flashes-of-chaos-guns-2026-11-12.md).
Page with GIFs and counts: https://claude.ai/artifact/7mmENE18CeByS1BaHeRt9K
(source `rounds-2026-11-12/index.html`, clips in `rounds-2026-11-12/gifs/`).

## What was built (CPU only)

`src/mould/rounds.rs`. A round is up to four cards read left to right into
casts. Payloads: blast, gas, germ (the squad toolkit's tools). Modifiers: wide
(radius x1.6), scatter (3 smaller bursts), quiet (no alarm). Triggers: delay
(lodges and fires 3 s later), thick (lodges and fires when 40 trail cells sit
within 5 cells, or after 30 s). A modifier or trigger applies to the next
payload. Each cast is a list of `Burst`s, the shape agreed with the squad
toolkit thread as its future `squad::Burst`; a test proves a blast burst does
exactly what the kit's blast does. Shots stay free; each payload costs its kit
price (blast 60, gas 150, germ 300); recharge is 2 s plus 1 per payload, 0.5
per other card, 1 per wide. Lodged casts cap at 64. `examples/rounds.rs` runs
the census and writes frames (`look=DIR`, `LOOK_FROM` seconds).

## Deciding measure (named before the run)

From the study: the best loadout differs on at least three tiers, and on every
tier beats random by a sign test surviving Holm.

## Census

Race world, farm hand on Defend, cap 300 s, seeds 5-12 paired, 8 loadouts.
Cut from 16 seeds to 8 and from 13 loadouts to 8 to save time after the owner
asked why the run took two hours. Raw output in `rounds-2026-11-12/raw/`.

| Plain | 0.223 | 0.249 | 0.224 | **0.589** | 0.237 | 0.234 | **0.572** | 0.231 |

| Sporer | 0.165 | 0.195 | 0.194 | **0.440** | 0.178 | 0.195 | **0.464** | 0.185 |
| Sleeper | 0.251 | 0.254 | 0.253 | 0.251 | 0.254 | 0.253 | 0.250 | 0.255 |

trap = thick+wide+blast, lull = quiet+gas+quiet+blast, plague =
germ+delay+blast. Drifter: stopped at seeds 5-7 to free cores (owner asked
for smaller runs); every loadout 0.347-0.360, nothing clear.

- Germ and plague beat no round 8/0 (p 0.0078) on plain, Swarmer and Sporer,
  and plague beats random 8/0 on all three. With 8 seeds 0.0078 is the
  smallest p a sign test can give, and Holm over 9 needs 0.0056, so **nothing
  survives the correction**; the 8/0 counts are consistent but uncorrected.
- Trap beats no round 8/0 on the Swarmer only. Blast vs none 7/1 on plain
  (p 0.07). Everything else is within 0.03.
- The Sleeper ignores every round (0.250-0.255).
- **The measure fails**: germ or plague is best on three of four tiers, so as
  tuned the cards are a better gun, not counter-play.
- Random is a weak control: 4 of its 8 draws had no payload card. It should
  redraw until the round has a payload.

## Frame cost (CPU, per tick, seed 5)

No round 3.7-5.9 ms; blast 5.1-6.6 ms (a blast turns the alarm field on);
shock 7.0 ms; germ 9.0 ms; plague 15.1 ms (germ plus blasts). The germ's cost
is the sickness field, as in the toolkit.

## What the clips show

At 240-300 s the plain mould keeps a large looped network. Blast, shock and
trap thin the network near the soldiers but it regrows; germ and plague leave
almost nothing (61 and 81 mould cells against 3380).

`BigParams::germ_resist` (a walker catches the germ only over `sick_catch` x
resist) set with `GERM_RESIST=` in `examples/rounds.rs`. Swarmer and Sporer,
seeds 5-8, 4 seeds only (the owner and both reviewers asked for small checks
and building over long census): raw `rounds-resist3.txt`, `rounds-resist10.txt`.

## In the window (playable, evening of 11-12)

The owner asked whether rounds were playtestable (20:23Z). Rounds now cast
through the squad toolkit's `squad::Burst` and `World::burst` (PR #159), so
they run on the GPU world, and `larder-world` takes them:

```
cargo run --release --bin larder-world -- rounds=trap
```

`rounds=` takes a loadout's name or its cards (plain, blast, wide, scatter,
quiet, delay, trap, shock, gas, lull, germ, plague, ambush); Y cycles the
loadouts this world can cast and says the recharge. Gas and germ are skipped
on the GPU world until the toolkit ports them (`Rounds::unsupported`). The
`Thick` trigger now counts walkers with `World::walkers_in` (exact on the
CPU, the frame's mould grid on the GPU) instead of its own occupancy scan; the
CPU tests are unchanged. A step that a command finishes (`Game::apply`) is not
seen by rounds, so its hits cast nothing.

Smoke check, headless on software Vulkan, Fork map, rush hand, seed 1: plain
shots lose all 4 soldiers by 20 s (mould 2,427); with trap the squad keeps 4,
19 casts kill 2,951 walkers for 1,140 food, and the mould is 420 at 20 s and 54
at 60 s. Strong on this seed: a tuning note under the build-first rule. The
per-step CPU work is the hit match; each cast is one `World::burst` (its cost
is #159's). No 1070 timing yet.

Re-run on main of 11-13 (the larger default mould of #164, about 24,000
walkers at 20 s): the squad is lost by 20 s with plain shots and with trap
alike; trap kills 2,418 walkers against 685 (8 casts, 480 food).

## Next

Owner's testing rule (2026-11-12 17:26Z): build first, balance later; the
germ's lead is a tuning note, not a gate. So the next steps are building, not
census: rounds in the window (a loadout picker at the larder), then the GPU
port behind the fixed shot buffer. Per-tier `germ_resist` (a tier key, the
tiers thread's call; for example 1 on the plain mould, 3-10 on Swarmer and
Sporer) is a tuning knob for later. If a census is ever wanted, random should
redraw until the round carries a payload.
