The grey thumbnail test

All sixteen planets at one second, in grey, small, before and after. The test from the art direction: each biome should be recognisable in a grey thumbnail.

Rows: planets 1-2, 3-4, 5-6, 7-8; seed 1 then seed 2 of each. In the game the ground kinds differ little in grey value, so the layout shows mostly through rock and soft rock; the ground's own look is the terrain shaders' work.

The layouts themselves

The same sixteen maps drawn straight from their files in false colour, so the layout reads apart from the shaders: rock light grey, sand tan, clay brown, barren grey-green, wet blue, soft rock beige, crystals cyan, nests orange rings, the landing a white square.

1

Barrens

Was: scattered barren patches that read as plain ground. Now: three wide barren bands right across the route, meandering, ragged at the edges. Each band starves the slime as it crosses.

2

Marsh

Was: small scattered pools. Now: big pools from one broad noise, so they run together, on clay banks. It crawls.

3

Canyon

Was: short rock stubs and mesas that read as debris. Now: two ridges right across the map, about 50-80 cells wide, each with one or two passes (sometimes one gated), and fewer, longer walls between bigger regions; no mesas. Chokepoints.

4

Dunes

Was: exact clay circles under the crystals. Now: islands a third bigger with ragged, noisy edges. It races, and holds on the islands.

5

Claypan

Was: mostly clay in soft patches. Now: clay nearly everywhere, cracked into polygons of plain ground, the cracks broken in places. It digs in.

6

Thicket

Was: straight gate bars and perfect arcs that read as UI. Now: every hedge, ring and gate a chain of short pieces that wobbles and changes thickness (11-21 cells). Walls to chew through.

7

Spires

Was: a few rock blobs. Now: up to 210 small pillars (radius 7-15 cells), each ragged, plus a few mesas. Cover the slime grows behind.

8

Core

Every ground at once, three nests, rings and gates; its soft rock grown like the Thicket's. The core world, the last of eight.

Every planet is checked before you land

A planet that fails a check is rebuilt from the next attempt. The checks walk the map the way a squad does: every crystal and slime nest reachable from the landing, a crystal close to the landing, the far nest across the map on foot, half the crystals' charge open without breaking soft rock. Over 400 planets (seeds 1-50, planets 1-8) all passed within four attempts, 368 on the first, and a planet takes 65 ms to build, once a level.
