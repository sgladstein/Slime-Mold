# Your own mould: a proposal (copy, 2026-11-12)

Copy of the Claude Doc https://claude.ai/artifact/LBDQgb8x9RoH6bE11G8H3m,
abridged to its decisions; the doc holds the full tables.

**Proposal.** The player owns a second species in the same world, and it
fights the enemy mould the way the model already makes species fight:
conversion, where the bigger local mass converts the smaller. The player
also picks its body form: a set of the species' own settings.

**Lessons it starts from.** The budget skirmish and Two Fevers snowballed
to one strategy (food split by head count, fed walkers win fights); sorted
species bodies hold their border and don't fight; one or two verbs felt
too simple (owner's playtests, 11-10); a single pull dial was a stat, not
a choice.

**Three shapes, put to the owner.** A, gardeners (soldiers feed, lay
roads for and cut the player's mould; recommended). B, mould only. C, a
bought ally with a form and a stance. **The owner chose A, 2026-11-12
04:45Z.**

**Verbs, each a field write.** Feed (energy at a spot), Road (the
species' trail along a soldier's walk), Cut (shots on your own mould),
Hunker (dormant crust), Rally (own alarm), Fly (spores), Form (settings).

**The gardeners build, first cut** (CPU world, behind a switch so the
one-mould game is unchanged): the player's mould starts on the larder
crystal; shots and the bite tell the species apart; Road and Feed by
soldiers; Cut as an order; conversion by walkers' presence; a three-way
score. Forms later, once the probe says which are worth offering.
