# Guns: each squad owns a rolled gun (2026-11-14)

The owner's answers, 16:46-16:47Z, to the gun thread's questions:

1. "The squad owns the gun."
2. "Each gun defines the budget and slot count and range, spread, rate of fire as actual numbers. There should be a handful of base gun types (fast and short, slow and far, wide slow (with default 2-6x multiplier), etc. but each stat is drawn from a distribution, so those are the average per gun type, but you could get better or worse. Sometimes you get lucky with a really good gun."
3. Later: "As you progress in the game, gun are more likely to have increased budget" and "probably better base stats overall".

Bullets with rolled damage and multipliers come in the next PR. The owner has not yet said whether payloads (blast, bomb, gas, germ) are bullets or modifiers. The thread recommended bullets.

## What was built

The new module is `mould::guns`.

- **`GunKind`**: four kinds, each with an average gun at depth 1.

  | kind | range (cells) | shots/s | spread (cells) | bullets a shot | budget | slots |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | rifle | 24 | 1.0 | 0 | 1 | 10 | 4 |
  | carbine | 15 | 2.2 | 1.5 | 1 | 8 | 5 |
  | long gun | 40 | 0.5 | 0 | 1 | 12 | 3 |
  | scattergun | 13 | 0.6 | 5 | 4 (2-6) | 9 | 3 |

  The rifle is the gun every soldier had before. A squad with no gun of its own fires `Gun::standard`, which is that rifle built from the game's `Rules`, so the campaign and every existing test play as before. On Fork seed 1 the rifle film's counts match the plain loadout's (655 killed, 156 shots).

  "Wide slow, 2-6x" was read as bullets a shot: a fan of 2 to 6, each with its own spread. A spread multiplier is the other reading, and the thread asked the owner which one they meant.

- **`Gun::roll(seed, depth)`** and **`Gun::roll_kind`** roll guns as follows.
  - Range, rate, spread and budget are each the kind's average times `exp(sigma z)`, with sigma 0.12, 0.15, 0.3 and 0.15.
  - Slots move one either way, a fifth of the time each.
  - The scattergun draws 2-6 bullets a shot.
  - Luck is in each roll (owner, 17:32Z: "A lucky gun happens due to each individual random roll ... I think it should be the former"). Each draw is lucky on its own, 1 in 6, and then lands 1.5 sigma or more on the good side: a slot more, or 5-6 bullets for a scattergun. Every draw is moved down by the mean lift, so the kind's numbers stay its average.
  - A gun is called lucky when it grades 1.15 or better, the 92nd percentile over 6,000 rolls (median 0.99, 90% 1.14, 99% 1.28). Before 17:40Z, a single 1-in-12 roll made every draw good at once.
  - Deeper planets raise the budget by 12% a planet, up to 2.5x. Range and rate rise by 4% a planet, up to 1.4x, spread falls by as much, and an extra slot becomes likelier.
  - Over 3,000 rolls the mean budget rises from depth 1 to 5 to 10, and depth 10 is 1.8 times depth 1.
  - `grade` is each roll against its kind's average at that depth (1 is average), for a shop's price.
- **`Squad::gun`**, **`Game::gun(q)`**, **`Game::arm`** and **`Cmd::Arm`** (with a text form for replays) put a gun on a squad.
  - A split's new half, or a soldier starting a new squad, takes the standard rifle and the army's round, so a lucky gun is never copied (`Squad::offshoot`). A merge hands a gun and cards to a squad that has none. This is the thread's default, and the owner was asked at 17:40Z.
  - Each squad also carries its own cards (`rounds::Deck`, `Squad::deck`, `Game::mods`, `Cmd::Load`). `Rounds` casts, recharges and lays trails per squad (`cards_of`, `casts_of`). In the squad mode, H loads the controlled squad's cards. A squad with none fires the army's round, which is the H loadout in the RTS round.
  - `Game::gun_range(q)` is per squad now. The mould's panic reach reads the farthest gun (`gun_range_max`).
  - `shot_gap` reads the gun's rate, and the firing code fires `shots` bullets with the gun's spread.
- **`rounds::fit_gun`** checks cards against a gun's own budget and slots. A round now reads up to `MAX_SLOTS` (6) cards.
- **`guns::Loadout { gun, cards }`** is what a squad carries between planets. The roguelike thread builds the shop on it.
- **The window**:
  - `gun=KIND`, `gun=KIND:SEED`, `gun=roll` or `gun=roll:SEED` arms squad 0 when the game starts. Rolls use the planet's depth with `map=planet:D`.
  - **Shift+H** rolls a test gun for the controlled squad, cycling rifle, carbine, long gun and scattergun.
  - The loadout strip shows the gun's icon before the loadout name, as many slots as the gun has, and "Cost N of B" against the gun's budget. Its tooltip names the gun and its numbers.
  - There are new icons: `Carbine`, `LongGun` and `Scattergun`, plus the old `Rifle`. `draw::gun_icon` gives the roguelike shop the same icons.

## Checks

- **guns tests**: the standard gun is the old rifle, rolls are deterministic, rolls scatter round each kind's average and stay in bounds (and the text form round-trips), lucky is 1 in 12 and better, deeper means bigger budgets and better stats, and each kind is what the owner named.
- **Game tests**:
  - an unarmed squad fires its rules' rifle;
  - each squad fires its own gun and a split half keeps it (the carbine fires 4.4x as often as the long gun, and panic reach follows the farthest gun);
  - a scattergun fires a fan of bullets a shot;
  - `Cmd::Arm` round-trips as a line.
- The full suite, clippy and the wiki lint pass.

**Frame cost**: one `Game::gun` lookup a soldier a frame, and a scattergun's extra bullets. Not timed on the 1070. A long gun's 40-cell sight disc, or 120 cells with two Range cards, is the thing to time.
