Planets 4 to 8: where the slime has been

Planets 1 to 3 each had one look only they have: the meadow's patches, the barrens' worn dust tracks, the fen's wet slick, the last two drawn from the slime's trail on bare ground. Planets 4 to 8 had none. Now each biome shows that same trail its own way, fading as the trail does, so you can read where the slime has been from the ground. Before is on the left and after on the right. It is the same game on both sides, and only the view shader differs.

How it works

  • All five read the slime's trail (trail_lin) on bare ground, after the barrens' and fen's own, in shaded: SCUM*, WILT*, GRAVEL_FILL*, CAVE_GLOW, CORE_FLORA_GLOW.
  • The water mask is water_share, not a per-cell test: the first cut stepped the scum's edge on the cells of a deep pool.
  • Under the slime's film nothing changes: the slime covers the ground as before.

Tried and dropped

  • The highlands' gravel polished (a lift and pin glints): unseen at zoom 6.
  • The caves' phosphor as a wash under the pins: it ringed the body in a green halo, the kind the sheets and cords had just lost.
  • The jungle's wilt as the plants' colour scaled toward yellow: dark greens only went brighter green.
  • The core's flora glow at nearly twice today's: a magenta halo round the body.
