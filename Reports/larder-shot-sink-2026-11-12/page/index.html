Shots that sink into the slime

You found that a squad standing in one spot barely dented a large mass, while walking in tore it up. A shot stopped at the first slime on its line and centred its blast there, on the body's edge, so about half of each blast landed on open ground. Now the blast sinks one strike radius further along the line, onto the last slime within that reach. Against a thin strand it stays on the strand. Each clip below is the same fight twice, the old rule on the left and the new one on the right, with the counts running above each side.

What it doesn't change

  • Thin strands. Four soldiers that stop by the larder and fire at the wisps in range (the stand hand, 22-40 s) kill 5.5 a shot before and 5.2 now. The blast stays on a strand rather than overshooting it.
  • Walking in is still deadlier a shot. The gap was 42.0 against 12.6 (3.3 times); it is now 47.9 against 20.3 (2.4 times). A squad that stands still empties the slime in its reach and the craters keep it empty, while a squad walking in keeps meeting fresh slime, and pays for it in the slime's bite and the slowdown.
  • Craters are unchanged: 4.5 s, the cone. Your pick between crater=2 and scorch_keep=0.5 is still open.
