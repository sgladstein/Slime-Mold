Does cutting it off from its crystals pay?

At 2:30 a ring is cut round a stretch of C's tube between two crystals and held for the rest of the round, so the piece inside has no food. All three films show the same round: shape C on Fork, seed 1, with the same farm hand and the same cut. They differ in one food rule only. The key is kept off this page.
