How these were made

  • The window's own picture, from larder-world look=DIR hand=farm seed=1 cut=150,386,239,22 on software Vulkan. The ring is 6 world cells wide, 22 out from its centre.
  • "Walkers inside" counts the walkers inside the ring each second, from the engine's own fields.
  • "Mould on screen" is the share of the map's pixels drawn as mould, counted from the whole-map frames.
  • In both films the piece stays whole for a while, then dies within about 10 seconds. It does not thin out gradually.
