Shape C · cut off and scared

A cut-off part panics

You asked for a cut-off part to "act more like a scared animal. Either run away back to the main body or go right for the soliders. It should be more reckless", with no stronger attack. A part that has heard no news of food for 5 seconds now heads straight for the nearest soldier or building within 20 cells, or else the nearest crystal that still has food. For the first 1.5 seconds it goes at its own pace, which is the warning you can see. Then it runs at twice its pace. Its bite is the same as before.

What to look at

  • Does the charge read as a scared animal going for the soldiers?
  • It is on in shape C now, so the window plays it. larder-world -- panic=0 plays C without it.

How to beat it

  • A charge: shoot it before it closes. Four soldiers' shots stopped nine in ten of it; the few that got through did the damage. More guns, or standing further off, wins.
  • A run for a crystal: a wall in its way stops it, and it bites the wall. A squad on its route makes it charge instead. If it reaches the crystal, it feeds, calms and regrows there.

What it changes before any cut

The squad's own shots knock small bits off, and those bits now charge the squad too. Only a few walkers are scared at any moment (0 to 16 of about 15,000 to 27,000 in a CPU game on Fork), but each bit is right next to the guns. In the window (Fork, seed 1, the farm hand) the squad is worn down from 1:10. By 1:50 it has one soldier left and stops harvesting, while today's squad is back to 14. On the CPU world (4 seeds; idle, farm and strike hands; 5:00 each, before the wading change) the scores barely move. The idle hand still loses every game, and all 12 games run the full 5 minutes.

How these were made

  • The window's own picture, on software Vulkan, all four films on one build (today's, with the wading change): larder-world look=DIR hand=farm seed=1 cut=150,365,240,24 cutsquad=365,256,150,4 (scene 1) and cut=150,386,239,22,8,16 (scene 2), each with panic=0 (today) and the new default.
  • The scenes are staged: the ring and the soldiers' places are set by the command, not played.
  • The rule acts before the cut too, so each pair's rounds differ by 2:30: the mould has 18,512 walkers today against 22,825 scared, and scene 1's scared squad had one soldier left, made up to four.
