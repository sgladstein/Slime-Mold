How these were made

  • The window's own picture, from larder-world look=DIR hand=farm seed=1 on software Vulkan. There is a frame every 5 game seconds from 0:20 to 5:00, played at 6 frames a second.
  • The farm hand buys soldiers and harvests the crystal nearest the larder. Its squad is gone by 3:00 in one film and by 3:45 in the other. The idle hand loses its squad at about 1:45 in both.
  • "Mould on screen" is the share of the map's pixels drawn as mould, counted from the frames.
  • The films were made before the shot sink (#181) reached main. That changes how shots hit, not how the mould feeds.
  • A third rule was tried and is not shown: fed walkers packing two to a cell. It gave 21,053 walkers at 5:00, but only 7.6% mould on screen, because the drawn thickness is already full at about one walker a cell.
