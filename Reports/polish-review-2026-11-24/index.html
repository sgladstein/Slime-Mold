What still looks unfinished

A polish review of the whole game as it stands on main (build 406c2c9, 11-23). Fifteen findings, worst first, each with a fix and the lane that owns it. The two that matter most are the slime and the crystals at the zoom you play at: from far out the slime is glossy and alive, but at zoom 6 it turns into a soft orange wash, and the crystals read as flat clip-art cubes. Nothing here is built yet, except one key-name bug.

1

The slime is soft and smeared at the zoom you play at

Big lookLook and interface

From far out the slime is glossy and ridged. At zoom 6, planet 1's Drifter is a flat orange wash with a wide glowing edge and perfectly round blurred grey discs in it, and planet 3's Trunk trail is a flat translucent yellow sheet (next picture). It is the star of the game and the least crisp thing on screen, against art direction rules 4 and 5.

Fix. Carry the thick body's lighting (slope from walker density, one key light, a highlight) into the thin film from zoom 4 up. Cap the soft edge at 2 screen pixels. Break the round discs into the walkers' own sparse grain or ragged holes. Give the Trunk's trail a lit edge and a darker core. Show it on a before and after page.

2

Cell stairs where slime meets water

Look and interface

In the planet 1 picture above, where the Drifter fills a river bank its edge steps cell by cell down the bank. Rule 4 says no cell stair at any zoom. The slime probably stops at the water cell by cell while the shore is drawn from the smooth soil spline.

Fix. Clip the slime's shading with the same spline the shore uses.

3

Crystals look like clip-art cubes

Big lookLook and interface

Three flat-shaded faces, the shape of an isometric cube icon, the same on every planet. Often the largest and brightest shape on screen, with nothing where it meets the ground.

Fix. Keep the hexagon you ruled. Add a bevelled rim, a darker inner table, a few inner fracture lines, cyan light pooled on the ground by its charge, and an occasional glint. Consider a smaller drawn size, or a cluster of shards on the same footprint.

4

A spent crystal reads as a grey umbrella

Look and interface

Twenty-four flat grey trapezoids in a spoked hexagon, as large and as tall as a live crystal. It pulls the eye as much as a crystal that matters.

Fix. Lower and darker: a few broken shards and dust at ground level, thinner spokes.

5

Squad marks clutter the fight

Look and interface

Health bars show from 95% health, so a big squad wears a bar on every soldier most of the time. A spread squad draws two or more overlapping rings. The "1" label and the formation mark sit on the ring's line.

Fix. Bars only under 70% or for 2 s after a bite, with the squad's health on its tile. Merge overlapping rings into one outline. Put the label on a small dark plate inside the ring.

6

The bottom HUD reads as a developer's panel

Look and interfaceSquad and arsenal

Four empty unlabelled boxes beside "Rifle" and an unexplained H button. A lowercase status line ("blob, tight · fire: defend · no orders queued · Loadout 1 · 13 tools"). Labels broken over two lines with a hyphen ("Attack- / move", "Fire: / defend"). Key letters about 8 px in the corners. A small minimap jammed in the corner, and a lone "11" pill floating over the squad tile.

Fix. One HUD pass: empty gun slots collapse to one line saying where cards come from, H labelled Loadout, the status line as icon chips, one-word command names, key letters in a keycap, a framed minimap half again as big.

7

The command card's pages and icons

Look and interfaceSquad and arsenal

Holes in the grid. Back sits in column 5 on two pages and column 6 on the third. One letter means different things by page (B is Buy soldier, Mortar, Blast or Open; G is Drain, Door or Germ). On planet 1 the Tools page is five locks. Icons: Sleep gas is a pause sign; Blob and Tight use the crystal and currency mark; Turret power and Cheaper tools share a chevron; the Lure is a water drop; the Mine is the same target ring as Fire: defend.

Fix. A fixed grid with Back always bottom right. Locked tools hidden behind one "more tools in the shop" tile. One small icon set drawn for this game at one stroke weight.

8

The shop's counter and cards

Look and interfaceEngine and toolsDirection

Five cards fill a band across the middle; about 45% of the counter is blank, and the planet underneath shows through behind the bag row. The cards show different rows: payload cards show multipliers (Speed x1.8), gun modifier cards repeat the whole gun's stats, identical on every one (Damage 72, Fire rate 0.8/s, Range 20, Speed 36, Aim 7.0), plus "no room" once the gun is full. Building and tool cards are one line on a tall card. A Dev mode tab is in every run's shop. The planet-ahead map is a blocky mosaic.

Fix. Larger cards, or the next planet's story under them. An opaque counter. A card shows only what it changes, as before and after ("Damage 10 → 16"), with the gun's own stats once in the loadout strip. Dev mode behind an Options switch. A smoothed map.
