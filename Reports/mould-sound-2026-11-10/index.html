The mould, heard

Every sound here is a count off the dish. Growth is walkers born per second, feeding is walkers on food, starving is walkers dying per second. Each tool sounds when its tally moves. No sound is drawn for the ear alone, the same rule as the picture.

These are rendered offline by the code the window runs, from the same dish and hand, so the window plays the same thing. Headphones help: sounds sit left or right where they happen on the dish.

What I need from your ears

  1. Is the growth and feeding bed too loud next to the tools? It sits near -28 dBFS and the tools poke out of it by a few dB.
  2. Any sound that feels wrong for what it is: the oats thunk, the salt, the knife, the join notes.
  3. On the PC, press S in the game to turn it off and on.

Listener cost: about 1 µs a frame (0.6–1.6 µs mean over seven runs), against a 5 ms frame. The voice bank runs on the audio thread. Sound on and sound off play the same game bit for bit (the dish hash test). Recordings 44.1 kHz stereo, 96 kbps MP3. Source: Reports/mould-sound-2026-11-10/.
