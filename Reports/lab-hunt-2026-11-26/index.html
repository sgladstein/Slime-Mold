The Slime's Hunt

Can the slime's own rules bring its body to a squad that keeps moving? Now it can: it follows the squad's trail and the whole body pours after it. But that alone still costs a kiting squad no soldiers, because the body arrives down one corridor into the guns.

What changed

Once a game second the game remembers where the soldiers stood. The slime smells that trail, with older places weighing as farther away, so a walker that meets the path follows it toward where the squad is now. A place older than about 20 seconds smells of nothing, so a squad that gets far enough ahead leaves a cold trail and the body stops. No clock or trigger: only a field the walkers lean down, like the lure's.

Before and after, planet 2

Run seed 1, the same kiting bot with 15 rifles, every scheduled threat off. 1:00 to 1:40 of game time at five times speed.

Also tried with the hunt, each with 0 bitten: a haste on the trail, a charge reach of 15, no flinch.

What it means

Reach was half the problem. The other half is that the body arrives one front at a time, through the narrowest place, into the guns: six rifles killed 73,000 walkers in five minutes and were never touched. A squad lost soldiers only when it walked into the body.

Recommendation

  1. Keep the hunt as the slime's reach. It comes for the squad from its own senses and gives up on a cold trail. On its own it costs no soldiers, so it is not the threat yet.
  2. Next Lab step: arrive as a mass. Walkers at the end of the trail hold just outside the guns' reach until the front is deep, then come in together on the alarm, wider than the guns can cut. That is the wave event's idea, driven by the trail instead of by the squad's kill rate.
  3. Or a balance answer: the plain rifle at a corridor is the wall, the same thing the 11-25 review found (the bot wins planets 1 to 3 with it). That one is Direction's.
