1. Setting: slime that is shot at hardens

Four rifles stand at the first slime and keep shooting (seed 4). With setting on, walkers that are shot at for long enough set: they stop moving and hold their ground, drawn in amber, and wake once the shooting stops. Without it, the slime under fire behaves as today.

Do you want setting on or off?

2. Set slime: wall or trap

Setting on in both. Eight rifles rush straight into the body (seed 1). In one panel set slime is a wall: soldiers can't step on it and walk round it. In the other it is a trap: soldiers can walk in, but wade at 5% of their speed while it keeps biting.

Should set slime stop soldiers, or hold them?

3. Old ground: does the slime keep to it, or move on?

No squad in play (seed 1). The ground the slime has held keeps a crust. In one panel the slime is drawn back onto its old ground; in the other it is pushed off it toward ground it has never held.

Which slime do you want: one that keeps to its ground, or one that moves on?
