Gas and Blast now endanger your own squad

Following the owner's Noita rule ("Any single spell that is incredibly powerful is also really dangerous"), two strong single cards now carry a risk to your own squad. Gas's cloud puts your soldiers to sleep, and a Blast hurts the soldiers inside its kill disc.

On by default. Soldiers and turrets firing on their own hold a Gas or Blast that would land on the squad and fire a plain shot instead, so the danger comes from your own aimed volleys and from clouds drifting back over you. The clips below are aimed casts. On planet 3, 2:00 in, the greedy squad kept 1 and 11 soldiers with Gas (no danger: wiped, and 21), and 8 and 13 with Blast (no danger: 13 and 20).

Planet 1, run seed 1, scripted greedy squad of 15 draining a crystal. At 40 s one gas or blast round goes off on the squad's spot (boom=40,226.6,52.1,CARDS). Frames every half second from 38 s to 56 s, played at 4 frames a second (2x speed). Software GPU, filmed headless.

Blast · RoundRules::blast_hurt 40

A blast takes 40 health off every soldier in its disc

Shots stop at the first slime, so a blast reaches your soldiers only when it goes off in slime that is on top of them. Firing blasts into a body that has reached the squad now costs you health.
