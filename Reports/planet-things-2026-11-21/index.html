What each slime does by minute 2

Exit test 4 of Milestone 1: after playing, Scott can say in a sentence what each slime did to him. One clip per planet. Each is the run on main as of 11-21 (after the pacing, #606 and #644), the greedy or pumps bot, software Vulkan, zoom 6. Clips play a game second in 1/8 s (planet 3: a half second).

Planet 1 · the Drifter

It smells your pumps, streams out to them and eats them.

Run seed 1, pumps hand, 0:26 to 1:15. The slime eats 103 food and surges at 0:29; a gold arm runs from its crystal to the near pump, which goes from 194 to 0 health by 1:10 and is rebuilt. The planet card says it: "It smells your pumps and comes to eat their crystals". Fruiting no longer plays here (#644), so this is planet 1's one thing.

Planet 2 · the Sprawler

Its fast threads find your squad from far off, and mini-bosses come out of it.

Run seed 2, greedy hand, 1:18 to 2:00, the camera on the squad. Thin streaming threads cross the squad's path from every side; a hunter of 316 walkers forms at 1:16 (the violet body on the rock edge late in the clip sits where the log puts it). On seeds 1 to 3 a mini-boss formed by 1:25 on 2 of 3 and a pod (fruiting) by 1:30 on 2 of 3. Strikers, at 0.6, formed on none of the three by 3:00.

Planet 3 · the Trunk

Cut down, its edge condenses into gold strikers that charge your squad.

Run seed 1, greedy hand, 1:26 to 1:46, the camera on the squad. Under 60% of its peak at 1:28, the edge facing the squad condenses: 60 strikers at 1:30, 413 at 1:39, 994 walkers taken in by 1:50. On seeds 1 to 3: strikers by 1:30 on 2 of 3, a mini-boss by 1:40 on 3 of 3 (a Pinwheel on seed 3). Waves and the late lunge come later in the planet. The amber walls round its crystals (the card's line) show from the landing.

Also in this change: a pod's burst no longer rains slime on the squad (PR #648). Its throw of 600 fine cells, wider than a planet 1 map, now stops short of the guns' reach from the nearest soldier. Planet 2 run seed 3: 8 soldiers left at 3:00 before, 23 after.

Seen while filming: on planet 2 seed 2 the hunter sat pressed on a rock edge from 1:44, 50 to 100 cells from the squad, and never reached it.
