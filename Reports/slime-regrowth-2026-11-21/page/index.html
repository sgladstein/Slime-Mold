What kept the slime at its peak

The slime was never short of food. Its size on this planet is set by its slots, not its food: it starts with 14,320 walkers and has room for 28,640, and it reached that ceiling at 3:44, 11:04, 14:23 and 16:27. One fully covered big crystal pays the upkeep of about 24,000 walkers, so a single big crystal keeps it near the ceiling. After you cut it to 11,400 at 8:13 it was back at the ceiling by 11:04, because it eats about three times its upkeep and the spare goes into births.

More than one crystal was still feeding it. The log ends with 401,000 charge on the map, and in the last minutes, when you took nothing, the map lost 450 to 560 charge a second. A fully covered big crystal gives at most about 150 a second, so three or more crystals were still being eaten, or partly drained ones still held a lot. Nothing else fed it: drained crystals fall to crumbs (0 to 350 charge), starfall ore and cores only fill its surge meter, and plants are not food on a crystal planet.

Big, average and small crystals, all planets

A pump pays by the crystal's food; the slime eats by its area. So big crystals are worth far more to you than to the slime, and small ones are worth almost the same to you as a median one, because over half of all crystals pay the 5-a-second pump floor. Measured on 40 run seeds a planet.
