Late in a planet the slime condenses and charges

Your pick on the balance card was "Condensed strikers + late lunge", and this page is the strikers half. Once the slime is under 40% of its biggest, its edge facing your squad condenses. Each condensing walker takes in up to three neighbours and becomes a bright gold bead with a slow pulse. A bead takes one shot for each walker it holds, plus one, and bites as hard as all of them together. Every bead charges your nearest soldier. No slime is added, because what a bead takes in, the slime loses.

What it is made of

  • Where: once a game second, each walker that can walk to within your guns' reach plus 12 cells of a soldier has an even chance to try. Slime across deep water or behind rock never tries. A first version used straight-line distance instead. On planet 3 that left 2,000 strikers stuck on a river bank for a minute while the squad shot across.
  • How: in each small block of ground, the first walker that tries takes in up to three of the others. Those walkers are gone, and their weight is now its own.
  • Why it is harder: one shot kills every plain walker in its blast. Four walkers merged into one bead take four shots. The same slime crosses your guns four times as tough, and it is coming at you.
  • The look: each bead is the walker itself, drawn as a lit gold drop. It grows with what it holds, and its glow brightens and dims on a slow pulse. A crowd of them reads as a hot front. There is no bronze and no outline, and the look is open to change.

Also in this change

The fruiting knot and the waves now take turns: neither starts while the other is under way. The review lane saw almost every fruit come with a wave. The heartbeat also goes in with this change, so the slime's edge swells and ebbs every 6 seconds.
