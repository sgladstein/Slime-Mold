# The game UI over the Larder world (2026-11-11)

**Built:** a game UI for the world view (`slime_mould::world::{canvas, ui,
icons, demo}`), asked for by the owner on 2026-11-11 20:16Z: "We need to know
about solider health, numbers, ways to control them, eventually we will have
different weapons, upgrades, build walls (and probably other things in the
future), menus, enemy info", and "make it look modern and professional."

Page: [world-ui-2026-11-11/page.html](world-ui-2026-11-11/page.html) (listed
in [pages.md](pages.md)).

## What it is

- **A canvas pass** (`canvas.rs`, `canvas.wgsl`): screen-space shapes
  (rounded panels with gradients, borders and holes, soft shadows, bars,
  rings, and atlas masks for text and icons) binned on the CPU into 16 x 16
  tiles; one workgroup a tile walks its list in draw order and blends. It
  runs after the view's compose, on the same `out` buffer. Marks were not
  used: a mark claims a pixel outright and cannot blend text over glass.
- **Type and icons:** Inter Medium and SemiBold (OFL, `assets/fonts/`) through
  `ab_glyph`; 30 line icons drawn for this game in a 24-unit box and
  rasterized with `tiny-skia`. A 1024 x 1024 coverage atlas, emptied and
  refilled when full.
- **The HUD** (`ui.rs`) has no game rules: the game fills a `Model` each frame
  (resources, clock, objective, selection cards, an info panel, a hover card,
  commands with slot, hotkey, cost, cooldown and target kind, ghost validity,
  end screen) and gets `HudEvent`s back. It takes the mouse and keys first and
  says whether it used them. Top bar, alerts with a world and minimap ping,
  group and idle chips, a selection panel, a 4 x 3 command card with
  tooltips, a placement ghost (green or red, with the cost or the reason by
  the cursor), a pause menu, a controls page and an end screen.
- **Camera inset:** the world scrolls out from under the top bar and the
  bottom panels; the minimap sits in the UI's frame (`View::minimap_box`).
- **Stand-in squad** (`demo.rs`, moved out of `mouldgpu.rs`): 12 soldiers
  (rifles and flamers), 6 rivals that fire back, stances, weapon swap,
  recruit, turrets, walls by length, an armour upgrade on a timer. No game
  rules: it exists to drive every panel.

## The independent review

An agent reviewed commit d58fc64 cold (code and renders, no conclusions
given). Big picture: the compute canvas is a reasonable choice over egui
(which would bring its own pass, fonts and look); the `Model`/`HudEvent`
split is the right shape and the gaps are additions, not a rewrite. It found:

- Releases swallowed over panels (a drag-box, pan or minimap drag ending on
  a panel stuck). Fixed: a release is the UI's only when its press was.
- A wall or burn line released over a panel was lost. Fixed.
- Placements on the minimap went to the world behind it. Fixed: they land on
  the point the minimap shows (`Hud::aim`).
- `ghost_ok` was ignored on placement. Fixed: refused with the reason, and
  targeting stays.
- The end screen let keys through; Space fell through to pause when no alert
  had a place; targeting ended silently when energy dipped mid-drag; group
  chips counted the dead; a tooltip `clamp` could panic. All fixed; the new
  tiny-window test found a second `clamp` panic (the minimap frame), fixed.
- Canvas overflow kept each tile's first shapes (the panels) and dropped
  text and menus; the atlas never emptied, so resizing through many sizes
  could make new text vanish. Fixed: buffers grow; a full atlas is emptied
  for the next frame; the UI scale steps by 0.05 (cap 2.4).
- Look: enemy info swapped the selection panel on hover (now a hover card);
  the cooldown ring read as a broken border (now a dark wipe with the time
  left); 12 px weapon badges (now 17 px); Hold and Defend were both shields
  (Defend is an eye); the idle moon read as night (a soldier with a z); the
  flamer looked like a camera (redrawn); group chips did not say which
  number was which (key, soldier icon, count).

Not done, from the review: command pages or submenus for when weapons and
buildings outgrow 4 x 3; selectable buildings; a production queue;
Shift-queued moves and rally points; several bars and status effects in the
info panel; unit blips on the minimap; a settings menu; DPI scale from the
window. Alert pings and the ghost are drawn as world marks; they are UI, not
simulation, and are left there for now.

Tests: 12 new (`ui.rs` 10, `canvas.rs` 2): release pass-through for each
button, a wall released over a panel, placement through the minimap, a red
ghost refused, end-screen modality, Esc through the menu pages, Space, tiny
windows, the scale step, a full atlas, unlimited binning.
