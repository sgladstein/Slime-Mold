The looks before and after the food-tubes census (2026-11-11/12), one
script and output folder each. The scripts were run from a scratch folder
($SP) with scratch builds of examples/bigdish.rs: look1-2 before the
`joined` line existed (no joined counts), look3 on, `joined=1`. Pictures
were looked at, not kept, apart from the page's strips (../pics/) and
race-s3-090s.png (the census's slowest seed at 90 s).
- l1 (look1.sh): food reach, network, explorers, crystals; 1024 x 640, 60 s.
- l2 (look2.sh): growth paid for by crystals from a small start; explorers, energy flow.
- l3 (look3.sh): growth with energy flow; short explorer stages.
- l4 (look4.sh): the race world's numbers; explorers, energy flow.
- l5 (look5.sh): growth from the droplets' wide disc; droplets on the race world.
- l6 (look6.sh): race without growth, and from a 25,000-walker start.
- racecry-s1.txt: the dropped arm's seed 1, rerun; its end hash is race's.
