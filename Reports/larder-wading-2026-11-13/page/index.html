Wading into thick slime

Soldiers in thick slime now slow right down. The slowdown counted each walker at shape C's weight of 0.2, which was meant to keep the 25,000-walker mould from overwhelming buildings and the larder. So 30 walkers had to be on a soldier before it slowed to half speed. Now every walker counts whole, as it already did for the bite. Both clips are from today's game. The clips I showed you before were cut on a build from before the bite doubled (PR #174), which is why that squad walked in unharmed.

Walking into the body

Eight soldiers leave the larder at 3 s and walk into the body toward the core. On the left the slowdown counts walkers at 0.2 each (the old rule); on the right every walker counts. Played at twice game speed, with the squad's counts above each side.

Standing at the edge and firing in

The same eight soldiers walk to the mouth of the right-hand channel, at the edge of the thick slime, and stand there on free fire from 6 s. They never walk in. On the left the blast lands on the first slime it meets (the rule before PR #181); on the right it sinks one blast radius in (the default now). Wading makes no difference here, since few walkers reach them.

This squad fired rarely: about 50 shots in 26 seconds from eight soldiers that could fire about 200. A soldier aimed at the densest slime in sight, found without checking for walls, and here that was behind the right-hand wall, so the shot was called off and the slime in front went unshot. That is fixed below.

Aiming only at slime it can see

The same stand, sink on. On the left, the old aim, picked through the wall. On the right, a soldier aims at the densest slime it can see, with a clear line to it, on both engines. Turrets aim the same way.

  • It fires at its full rate and kills almost twice as many. It shoots the slime coming at it, which is thinner, so fewer a shot, and ends healthier because that slime never reaches it.
  • The sink still isn't tested here: the thick body in the channel is just out of range (about 43 world units against 30). In this stand the sink makes no difference (2.6 a shot with it off). In a stand nearer the body it raised kills a shot from 4.7 to 5.3.

What I tried and dropped

  • Keeping a soldier's own blast off the walkers on it. I thought a soldier in the slime blasted its own feet clear. On today's game that isn't what keeps a squad alive: walking in, 16 to 28 walkers sit on each soldier anyway. You also pointed out that a soldier rushed while standing still could never shoot what reached it. So it is not in, and the code is kept in the report as a patch.

Try it
