# Probe 3: Firedamp, the gas is your dynamite

Read `common.md` beside this file first.
**Sources:**
- Firedamp in `explorer-a-flows.md` §1, `explorer-c-open.md` §4 and `explorer-d-cards.md` §2
- **Extensions:** `review-1-player.md` §4 Firedamp and `review-2-engine.md` §3–5
- **The merge it may become:** "Seep", `review-3-synthesis.md` §4a

**Pitch.** A mine keeps filling with gas that seeps from the rock and creeps toward the miners' lamps. Herd it with fans and doors, and burn it off in controlled pops. A blast is also how you get ore, so you are always weighing greed against safety.

**The question.** Is "too thick to burn, so thin it first" a rule a fresh player learns from one fizzle, and does herding gas feel crisp or mushy?

**Probe scope (2–3 days):**
- **The gas field on the fine grid**, inside your game: it spreads, drifts (it rises, or pools if it's heavier than air; pick one, and make the other a perk later), and escapes at an air shaft. Leaks feed it.
- **Do not route pushes through the air grid.** It works in 16x16 blocks and heat doesn't move it. A fan adds drift to the gas directly, inside its cone.
- **Three fixed colours**, strong over dark rock: too thin, burnable, too thick. This is the readability gate.
- **The burn band.** A flame front runs only through burnable cells, uses up the gas as it goes, and stops at thin, thick or wet cells. A big burnable pocket hands off to the shipped explosion code, with the blast size following the gas that was in it. Fracture can open new holes; skip that if it's costly.
- **Tools, no cards:**
  - **fans**, dragged to aim, which blow until picked up;
  - **doors**, tapped to slam or open live;
  - **matches**, thrown;
  - **fuse markers**, which light when the gas at that spot can burn.
- **Sound or flash carries the band.** A fizzle in thin gas, a "whump" that grows deeper the thicker the gas was.
- **Level:** one gallery shaped like a U, a sump with a leak, two work faces with lamps, an air shaft and an ore seam. The score is ore from blasts in 3 minutes. A face goes dark while gas is near its lamp, and a face that blew is lost. Stars.
- **Leave out for now:** the mould (Seep), the run and its perks. Mention them on the page as next steps.

**Measure:**
- how long doing nothing takes to lose (target about 40 seconds);
- planned against random over paired seeds;
- for a fresh player (or Scott), whether they can say where a flame will stop before they light it;
- the worst frame during a burn.
