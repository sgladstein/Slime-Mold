# Probe 1: lightning that leaves a mark ("Storm Glass" / "Arc with scars")

Read `common.md` beside this file first.
**Sources:**
- Arc in `explorer-a-flows.md` §2, `explorer-b-growth.md` §1 and `explorer-d-cards.md` §1
- Fulgurite in `explorer-c-open.md` §3
- **Extensions:** `review-1-player.md` §4 "Arc with scars" and `review-2-engine.md` §4 "Storm Glass"
- **Consolidated version:** `review-3-synthesis.md` §4b

**Pitch.** A storm cloud is over a valley. You call lightning down and steer it onto lamps, away from the village and the powder store. Every bolt leaves a lasting mark, and later bolts follow those marks, so over a storm you watch a wiring tree grow out of your own strikes.

**The question.** Is aiming lightning fun in the hand, or does it feel like dice?

**Probe scope (2–3 days):**
- **Skip the charge-field solve.** The bolt is a growing tip that weighs each neighbour by:
  - a downward pull;
  - how well that cell conducts, read from the fine cell itself (water and wet ground high, copper higher, dry rock low, glass zero);
  - a pull toward the nearest conductor, from a distance map built once per cast;
  - fresh ions.

  Raise the weight to a "straightness" power and add randomness, so the bolt forks.
- **Hold to call, release to strike.**
  - While you hold, the leader creeps down for about 1 to 1.5 seconds, with faint feelers ahead of it.
  - A longer hold gives a straighter, heavier bolt.
  - Hold too long and the cloud strikes by itself, on the tallest conductor.
- **Taps while it creeps:** one tap places a splash of water (or a pinch of copper), and the leader bends toward it as you watch.
- **Hits:**
  - fire on anything flammable;
  - water to steam;
  - sand or soil the bolt passes through becomes a glass or scorch trail that later bolts prefer (glass can start out as any existing solid);
  - ions laid on the fine trail field, fading over about 3 seconds.
- **Level:** a cloud, a bell tower (the tallest point, which you protect), one lamp in the open, and one lamp under an overhang. Score lamps lit against tower hits, with stars.
- **Leave out for now:** the copper network kept across a run, the card row and the charge solve. Mention them on the page as next steps.

**Measure:**
- the same marked target over 10 seeds, with hits counted;
- the do-nothing run (overflow strikes) against a planned run;
- the frame cost over a run of strikes, not over a single one.
