**New in this round: the card or wand mechanic is no longer required.** For any concept, you may say it is better without cards and propose the verb it should have instead: direct drawing, placing tools, a single spell, steering in real time, and so on. Say why.

## Your job
1. **Combine.** Find which ideas could take mechanics from each other, or merge into one stronger game. Be specific about what moves where. Example shape: "Firedamp's burn band plus Arc's bolt: lightning that only ignites gas in the burnable band, so you steer the storm onto a vent you have thinned." Propose at least three combinations. Judge each one honestly: say when a merge would only make a muddier game.
2. **Rank.** Produce a top 5 across all 21 concepts and your combinations. For each, give one paragraph on why it would be fun to play, and the single biggest reason it might not be.
3. **Extend your favourites.** For your top 2 or 3, add ideas the explorers missed: new verbs, new surprises, how a session or run is shaped, what the first 60 seconds look like, how it reads on a phone-sized screen, and a cheaper or sharper first prototype. Card-based or not, whichever is better.
4. **Flag what is wrong.** Name any claim in the reports you think is wrong or too optimistic, including about this engine. If you check a claim against `/home/user/Pixel_Physics/src/sim/` or `/home/user/Pixel_Physics/wiki/`, say what you checked. Verify at most three claims; read source only to confirm one specific claim, never to explore.

## What to deliver
Write one Markdown file to the path your role names, of at most about 3,000 words. Order it like this:
1. Your top 5, as a ranked list, one line each.
2. Combinations.
3. Ranking reasons.
4. Extensions.
