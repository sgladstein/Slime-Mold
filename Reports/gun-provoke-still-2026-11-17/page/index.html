Provoke and Still

The last two Mind cards. Neither kills anything. A Provoke bullet makes the slime round its hit believe it has been cut off from food, so it leaves what it was doing and charges the guns. A Still bullet calms the slime round its hit, so a charge walks past instead of biting. Both are on in the game now: the shop sells them as bullets from planet 2, and the gun range has them from the start.

In every clip the left half fires plain bullets and the right half fires the card in every other shot. Both halves are the same game up to the second the deck is loaded.

Provoke: pull the slime off a crystal

Planet 1. A squad of eight stands beside a crystal the slime's stream is feeding. Left alone it only defends itself, so with plain bullets the slime's film grows over the crystal next to it. With Provoke, the slime it hits hears no news of food. About five seconds later it turns grey, lets go of the crystal and runs at the squad, into the plain bullets between each Provoke.

plain, 16 to 32 s shots fired 23 slime killed 319 slime on the map 12,980 to 12,899 soldiers 8 of 8, health 98
Provoke + plain, 16 to 32 s shots fired 42 (21 of them Provoke) slime killed 387 slime on the map 12,980 to 12,608 soldiers 8 of 8, health 98

The crystal is clear at 27 s with Provoke and covered with plain. The squad fires almost twice as often because the slime comes into its reach. Some of the fall in slime is the provoked part withering by itself, as a part cut off by a wall does.

Still: take the bite out of a raid

Planet 1 again. Eight soldiers stand in the open as one group. The slime surges at 54 s and sends a raid at them at 59 s. With plain bullets the raid lands on the group. With Still, the raiders it hits lose the charge for ten seconds and drift past.

plain, 56 to 72 s slime killed 585 most slime on a soldier 13 soldiers 7 of 8, health 71
Still + plain, 56 to 72 s slime killed 320 most slime on a soldier 8 soldiers 8 of 8, health 87

Still is a trade. The group keeps every soldier and more of its health, and it kills about half as much, because half its bullets kill nothing and the calmed slime does not walk onto the guns.

  • Still left the squad better off in 4 of the 6 windows and worse in 1 (more soldiers, or as many with more health; 39 soldiers against 35 in all), and left more slime alive in 6 of 6. With six windows that is a lean, not a proof (sign test p 0.38 for the squad, 0.03 for the slime).
  • Provoke changed little here (more kills in 5 of 6, p 0.22; soldiers 35 and 35). In these windows the slime was already charging the squads, and Provoke has nothing to add to a charge. Its use is the first clip: slime that is sitting on something and will not come out.
  • On planet 2 at 70 s the whole squad died with every deck. That window is the slime's, not the cards'.

In the game, and what is not settled

  • Where to find them. The run's shop offers them as bullets from planet 2. The gun range's armory has both under the Mind bullets. Each costs 2 of the gun's budget.
  • Numbers. Both reach 8 cells round the hit. Provoke lasts 12 s and Still 10 s. These are first guesses.
  • Provoke brings the slime onto you. That is the point of it, and it will hurt a squad that cannot hold what comes.
  • Not timed on the GTX 1070. A cast is one pass, the same as Blind's.
