Front Doors and Back Roads

Every nest now sits in a rock bowl with two ways in: a narrow front door facing the landing, a squad's kill box, and a wide breach round the side, reached by a road over the ground. The play review found almost no nest with both. Every picture pairs the map fixes (before) with this change (after), same planet, same seed, with the counts beside it.

"Direct / round" under each picture is the reviewer's measure: the widest way within 15% of the shortest walk, then the widest way of any length.

What a nest looks like now

LooksPlayreview item 4

A lobed rock rim, 26-36 map cells thick, rings a bowl 104-136 across inside. The door is a channel 7-9 wide straight toward the landing. The breach is a gap 28-35 wide, 105-150 degrees round. The road, 20-23 wide, runs from the landing to in front of the door, then round the outside of the rim to the breach. It takes the cheapest way over the ground: rock costs 6 times plain ground and deep water 3 times, with noise so it wanders. Crystals, hedges, rings and the landing's crystal keep off the road, the door and the breach. The planet's check holds every nest to both widths, or the planet is built again.

Tried on the way

First-try passes on seeds 1-8 (64 planets) after each step.

  1. A rim, a door, a breach and a straight road. 42 of 64.
  2. The landing's crystal moved off the roads (it narrowed a highlands way round to 10): 43.
  3. Nest rings moved off the lanes (they shut the way round on every jungle): 53.
  4. The landing's crystal allowed farther, anywhere in plain sight, where the landing sits in a small room: 54.
  5. The road's arc starts at the door, not 60 degrees round, which was as quick a way to the breach as to the door: 58.
  6. Cut-off caverns filled: 60.
  7. Roads routed over the ground, not cut straight as boulevards through the caves and ridges: still 60, and the caves keep their tunnels.
  8. Dropping a nest under 400 apart on every map failed 7 of 20 river valleys' first tries; now only below today's area.
  9. Lobed rims, after the first frames showed drawn circles: 145 → 143 of 160.

Not done here, and next

  • Every rim is rock. A moat on the fens, a thorn hedge the squad can break in the jungle, or a ring of boulders on the meadows would tell the biomes apart (the looks review's item 3).
  • The door is 6 wide at its narrowest on the planet's check, 4 on the reviewer's. The channel is 7-9; the rim's knobs take the rest. A squad files through either way.
  • Crystal counts on planets 1-4 and the caves are down 4-11%.
  • Next: fens weighted to lakes, cave tunnels 5-15 wide, a landing crystal per biome, and nests kept off river crossings.
