Two Slimes

A second slime species that eats the first. The green hunter takes no food from crystals: it lives only on the orange slime it converts. One number, its edge, decides whether it eats the planet in two minutes, in five, or lives beside it.

From the model

The two species share the ground (one walker a cell, so bodies push), the crystals, the alarm and the squad. Each lays its own trail. The engine's only fight was conversion: a walker joins the species whose trail outweighs its own. Measured on 11-11 and 11-12, that stalls at an even split. Here the fight is one way, the green is drawn to orange trail and the orange turns from the green's, an orange walker turns green where green trail is at least edge times its own, and the green starves unless it converts. Converted walkers keep their energy, so the prey's food becomes the hunter's.

Recommendation

Keep the starving hunter as the second species. It is the first fight between two slimes that does not stall. Not for the game yet; three steps first:

  1. Start it small. Here it starts as half the walkers on a crystal it cannot eat, and nine tenths starve in 20 s. A small seed near the prey would show the chase without the crash.
  2. Make it a second enemy. Here it is the garden's green mould, so it gathers round the squad. In the game it would be a strain of the slime with its own colour, counted in the planet's slime, so one slime eating the other does not end the planet.
  3. The player's angle, the lab's next run: lead the orange into the hunter with the Lure and see whether a hunter at 1.0 then eats the slime faster than turrets do.
