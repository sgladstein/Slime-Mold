Two Slimes

A second slime species that eats the first. The green hunter takes no food from crystals: it lives only on the orange slime it converts. One number, its edge, decides whether it eats the planet in two minutes, in five, or lives beside it.

From the model

The two species share the ground (one walker a cell, so bodies push), the crystals, the alarm and the squad. Each lays its own trail. The engine's only fight was conversion: a walker joins the species whose trail outweighs its own. Measured on 11-11 and 11-12, that stalls at an even split. Here the fight is one way, the green is drawn to orange trail and the orange turns from the green's, an orange walker turns green where green trail is at least edge times its own, and the green starves unless it converts. Converted walkers keep their energy, so the prey's food becomes the hunter's.

Leading the slime into the hunter

Direction's next step: give the war a player's verb. A lure at the game's pull of 10 is laid in the hunter's ground, north-west of the orange body, from 2:00 to 7:00 (the pink rings). The hunter's edge is 1.0, where without help it only lives beside the orange.

Lure in the hunter's ground

At 5:00 6,714 / 7,395. At 7:00 8,247 / 6,651: the orange is 36% smaller, the green almost twice the size.

The lure draws the orange out of its body into the green, which converts it, so the front moves south twice as fast. Moved 15 cells further north-east the lure gives the same (7:00: 8,568 / 6,519). At edge 0.9, where the hunter barely lives, the lure takes it from 1,588 to 4,002 green and the orange from 13,168 to 11,989 by 7:00.

Two catches. The slime in all is no smaller: green and orange together are 14,898 with the lure and 14,605 without at 7:00, because the hunter is slime too. The lure turns orange slime into green slime; it pays the player only if the hunter is worth more to them than the slime it ate (it takes no crystal food, so a crystal it holds is free). And on run seed 2 the hunter starved to nothing by 2:00 with or without the lure, as it starts far from prey: the small-seed start is needed before this can be relied on.

Recommendation

Keep the starving hunter as the second species. It is the first fight between two slimes that does not stall. Not for the game yet; three steps first:

  1. Start it small. Here it starts as half the walkers on a crystal it cannot eat, and nine tenths starve in 20 s. A small seed near the prey would show the chase without the crash.
  2. Make it a second enemy. Here it is the garden's green mould, so it gathers round the squad. In the game it would be a strain of the slime with its own colour, counted in the planet's slime, so one slime eating the other does not end the planet.
  3. The player's angle (tried, above): the Lure leads the orange into the hunter and doubles the hunter's speed, but the slime in all is no smaller. For it to pay, the hunter should be worth something to the player, for example a strain that dies off once its prey is gone, or that the turrets render for more food.
