Four kinds of mini-boss

The hunter now has company. Each mini-boss draws a kind as it forms, among the kinds that have something to go for from where it is. Two come for you and two go after what you own. Every clip below is the same mini-boss on the same planet, forced to each kind in turn. The look of each kind is being made in its own thread; here they still render as thick slime, with a glossy knot where the body packs tight.

What changed for all of them

  • Which kind: drawn as it forms, evenly among the kinds that have a target from there. With no building up yet, only hunters and wanderers come; a piece cut off from food is always a hunter or a wanderer.
  • They no longer starve. The first sapper clip's sapper, pressed on its wall away from food, fell from 196 members to 47 in 6 s and broke with no shot fired. A mini-boss now lives on what it carries until it is broken or spent.
  • A fade when one ends: for 1.5 s its members stay marked, so the look thread can drain its colour before they go back to the slime.
  • Tighter at higher grades: the strongest pack a quarter tighter.
  • Both engines (the CPU and the graphics card) do all of this the same way; the card's version is checked against the CPU's.

Next

  • Mortar: stays on a crystal and lobs slime past your guns' range. Pinwheel: its members circle and sweep a squad. Both need new engine work.
  • Each kind's look: the look thread reads which kind each member belongs to, and whether it is fading.
  • The origin at each planet's start crystal, which the run's progression thread holds.

The best way to judge these is to meet them in a run: every kind is on in the run now, rarer and weaker on the first planets.
