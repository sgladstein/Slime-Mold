The hunter, built

Your answers to round 1, turned into the first mini-boss. It forms only where the slime crowds or where a big piece is cut off from food. It clenches, leaves as one body, hunts the nearest soldier, and takes many more hits than ordinary slime. How strong it is varies a lot from one mini-boss to the next. The look is being made in its own thread; in these clips it still renders like ordinary thick slime.

Inside the body it cannot be told apart from the slime round it today. That is the first job for the look thread: each member already carries a mark saying which mini-boss it belongs to, and how many hits it has taken, so the look can come straight from the simulation.

Weak and strong, the same seed

The same planet and the same moment, with the grade forced to its lowest and its highest. Each mini-boss draws its grade as it forms; the grade sets its size, the hits each member takes, how many walkers it touches a soldier as, and its pace.

The strong one wraps round the squad in a crescent while the guns carve out its middle, and lives. The weak one breaks up in 3 s. Everything between can come up: on planet 3 the grade runs from 0.11 to 0.71, and it rises deeper into the run (table below).

Under focused fire a packed body is easy to hit: each shot that landed hurt about 45 members at once. That is why the hits each member takes start at 4 and go up to 15. The pace bonus (up to 1.4 times) only made the strong one about 8% faster across the map, because a packed body moves as fast as it has room to, not as fast as each walker steps.

How often, planet by planet

Rarer and weaker early, more often and stronger deeper, with a wide spread on every planet. A crowding chance comes at a drawn time and waits for a crowded knot; a cut-off piece of 400 walkers or more gets its chance every second. The break-away specks are separate and stay more common early.

Next

The other kinds from the round 1 page, built on the same held body, a mix of ones that come for you and ones that stay put:

  • Wanderer: a cut-off piece that leaves for a far fresh crystal as one body and roots there.
  • Leech: sits on one of your pumps and takes its income until you kill it.
  • Sapper: packs against a wall between it and your pumps and eats a gap.
  • Mortar: stays on a crystal and lobs slime past your guns' range, so you have to push in.
  • Pinwheel: its members circle, so it sweeps a squad with spiral arms.

The ones that stay put will come from crowding on a crystal, and the ones that run from cut-off pieces, as the round 1 page planned.
