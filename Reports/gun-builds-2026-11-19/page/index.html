Gun Build Findings

Which card decks remove the most slime, and do the best ones follow the rule that strong builds come from combinations, often with rarer cards?

No. One common shell decides the ranking. Needle Blast (cost 4, from planet 1) is the base of every top deck in both tests. On its own it already removes +16k slime on planet 3 against a rifle with no cards. In the defend test it is within 2% of the best combo on planet 4.

Combos do add, but only to Needle Blast. Seek + Wide + Needle Blast adds 6k to 12k over Needle Blast alone on planets 3 and 4. No top deck needs a rare card. Germ is the only rare card that earns its slot, mainly when holding a crystal.

Full games

Does it hold in a real run?

The strategy bots played full planet 3 games with each deck bought as soon as it could be, on 9 maps including your own planet 3. With no cards the bot won 5 of 9. With Needle Blast it won 7 of 9. With seek + wide + needle blast it won 9 of 9, better on every map (sign test p = 0.004), in about 4:50 on average. It cleared your planet 3 in 4:05, against your 7:21.

Test 1 · one card each · seeds 1 to 6

Every single card

Median slime removed against the empty deck. Cards that need a payload were tested with plain Blast, and payload shells with each set. Several cards score exactly the same as Blast alone (Trigger, Thrift, Multicast, Expire), because a deck of one payload gives them nothing to act on.

On planet 2 the slime outgrows nine soldiers whatever they carry, so the differences there are small.

Scope

Not measured by this method

Both tests measure slime removed while shooting or holding still. Cards that only pay off with a plan of their own score near zero here. That says nothing about their worth.

  • Lures and movers: Bait, Provoke, Pull, Heave. Your split-and-hold play with Heave needs its own strategy. The strategy bots tried it, and it did worse on 7 of 9 maps for them.
  • Slow and stop: Still, Smother, Blind, Sour, Mist, Spin. Alone they did not keep slime off a held crystal either. They may need a damage card beside them.
  • Timing and chains: Delay, Trigger, Expire, Tinder with Torch, Shatter, Detonate. These need a second payload or fire on the ground to act on.
  • Turret builds were not tested.
Method

How it was measured

  • The gunlab= key in larder-world lands a run on a planet with one deck, bought at the shop's average grade for that depth. It refuses decks over the gun's budget or slots.
  • Each deck is compared with the empty deck on the same seeds on the same machine, because results differ slightly between machines.
  • The search went in stages: every single card, then pairs within card families, then 3 and 4 card guns from the best pairs on fresh seeds.
  • An independent review found 14 problems before the main runs. Among them, stalls were counted as wins and gas deaths were missed. All 14 were fixed in the harness first.
  • Raw rows and tables are in Reports/gun-builds-2026-11-19/ on the branch claude/project-thread-i0vg7o.
