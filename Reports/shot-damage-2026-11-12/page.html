After your answer: no stamp at all

You picked neither, because both were stamps. The same round now draws no mark at all. A hit shows only as the hole it really makes in the living body, and walkers flow back into that hole within about half a second.

Slowing the walkers barely helps, because holes fill from their rim. A hole that grows under fire and doesn't refill needs a rule: hit ground the mould avoids for a while. The Larder thread is testing that rule, and the picture will draw it from that ground.

What the simulation does when a shot lands

  • A shot stops at the first slime on its line. Its strike kills about 35 walkers in a disc 5 cells across.
  • The hole lasts about 0.2 game seconds. Walkers flow back into it from all sides, and the trail fills the hole back in by diffusion.
  • The mould regrows. Shots killed 1,456 walkers between 8 s and 16 s, but the mould only lost 610.

So a single hit is small and brief in the simulation itself. A picture can show it more clearly, but by the project's rule it can't invent damage that isn't there. Making hits last longer is a rule change, and that is being worked on in the Larder thread.
