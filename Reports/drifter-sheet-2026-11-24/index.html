The Drifter's sheet, before and after

Planet 1's slime (the Drifter) drags a thin gel of its own trail. Before, that gel faded over three or four cells and lay flat, so at zoom 6 the slime read as an orange wash with a wide glowing halo. Now the gel has an edge where the trail stands thick enough, and it rises a little, so the light runs over it and rims it. The picture is still the trail's own data: the edge is the trail's contour, about a cell wide, not a painted line.

What changed

  • The sheet is the trail where it stands over 0.15 to 0.3 (SHEET_EDGE, a smooth step), so its rim is a soft contour rather than a long fade.
  • It rises SHEET_RISE = 0.2 times its square, so the key light shades its swells.
  • Only the Drifter (planet 1's slime look) changes. Cost: two extra multiplies per cell in prep.
  • It bends the "no thresholded edge" ruling a little: the edge is a smooth step about a cell wide, on the simulation's own trail.

Tried and dropped

  • A raised floor of 0.25 or 0.6 under the body: one glossy slab that buried the body's ridges.
  • A rise alone (0.1, 0.2) with no edge: lit, but still a 40 px blur at zoom 12.
  • A colour cut on the slime's thickness: a speckled, ragged rim, because the thickness's tail is single walkers.
