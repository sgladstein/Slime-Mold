Crater looks in motion

The same fight four times, every game tick from 7 to 19 seconds, played side by side from one clock. Three are the blind crater looks from the Craters Under Fire page; the fourth is the game without craters. The counts under each clip follow the frame.

7.0 s

What you are watching

  • One fight, held still. The Fork, seed 1, the rush hand (all four soldiers sent at the mould's core), drawn by the GPU engine as the window draws it. The camera stays in one place (centre=256.5,272) instead of following the squad.
  • 7 to 12 s: the squad walks down into the body, firing as it goes. 12 to 15 s: it stands on the crystal and fires into the body above it. About 15 to 16 s: the body closes on the squad and it dies. This build has the core's pull (PR #126), which draws the whole mould onto soldiers near its core; without it the same squad lasts to 17-19 s. After 16 s there is no more fire, so you see how each hole behaves once the shooting stops.
  • Real time is 1× (15 game ticks a second, one frame a tick). Use ½× or ¼× to follow single hits, and drag the slider to step through frames.
  • The brown inside a crater is the ground (clay on the Fork) showing through where no walker stands. Nothing is painted on; every pixel is the simulation's own fields.

Looks 1 to 3 are the same three as the stills on the Craters Under Fire page, in the same order. The key is kept off this page. The looks differ in their firing too, so their shot counts differ.
