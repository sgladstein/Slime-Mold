Shots that carve a hole

You asked for a group of soldiers firing on the slime to carve a bigger and bigger hole that doesn't refill while they shoot. Each hit already left an alarm on the ground around it, a field in the simulation that fades over a few seconds. Now the mould turns away from fresh alarm. Under steady fire the hole stays open and the hits push deeper. When the shooting stops, the alarm fades and the mould flows back within about ten seconds.

Each clip below is one game on the race world. The mould grows for 60 s. Then four soldiers (the white squares) are set down 8 cells from the mould's edge. They fire for 20 s, then stop. The clips run three frames per game second. There is nothing drawn on top except the soldiers and the clock.

One question: A or B?

A and B differ only in how strongly the mould turns away from fresh alarm. One of them is in the game now. Which feels better under fire? You can also answer "neither" or "stronger still".

Free shots and the soldiers' scent

These censuses are 16 full games (seeds 5-20, 15 minutes each) per hand, run before the hole change. Free shots took the rush from losing every game to reaching the clock on 15 of 16, with the mould cut from 5,000 walkers to under 600 on 12 of them. It never finishes the mould, probably because the last stragglers lay too little trail to stop a shot (not yet checked). Farm and wall still beat idle and random. A stronger soldier scent (4 times as strong, twice the reach) brought the first touch on farmers from 49 s to 27 s. It also made random play stronger, so farm and wall beat it only 12 times of 16. 32 seeds on the race world and two designed maps are running now with the hole change in.

Cost

The alarm field is a 3 x 3 mean over the ground every step. On the CPU that adds about 2 ms to a 5.4 ms game tick. On the GPU it switches on the life passes the mould tiers use, which cost about 10 ms a tick on the cloud's software GPU at 100,000 walkers. The GTX 1070's figure is still open.
