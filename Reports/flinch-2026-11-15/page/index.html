Should the slime flinch when it's shot?

Every shot lays an alarm in the ground where it lands. Until now the default slime ignored it, so a squad firing into the slime stood in a sea of it. With the flinch, the slime's walkers turn away from fresh alarm. The alarm fades quickly, so the slime only gives ground while the shots keep coming, then closes back in. Here are four strengths of the same fight, from no flinch to a long one. Which one feels best to play against?

The squad meets the slime on planet 1

Run seed 1, planet 1, the squad firing into the slime as it arrives, 11 to 19 s at the game's own pace, the camera on the squad. Where the slime has a flinch, the pale glow round the squad is the alarm itself, at the haze strength the build now draws. Under each clip: soldiers left at 30, 60, 90 and 120 s, then the slime killed and the slime's size at 2:00.

These counts are one game each. Two of these flinches were also played against no flinch on five seeds of planet 1 with the same hand: each left the squad fewer soldiers at 2:00 on three seeds and more on one, which is no steady change in who wins. So the choice is how it looks and feels, not how hard it is.
