Q1. sensor_offset (reach) is 9. Shorter reaches were measured on all five dishes over 8 seeds and made every planned solution worse. Beat the Ruler's planned hand went from 33333333 to 00000000 at reach 6 (and 7), and Behind the Wall fell to 20031231. At reach 6, Keep It Alive's lamp+oats+water hand lived shorter on 6 of 8 seeds (median 6:21 -> 3:41), and The Wild One's all-tools share fell from 0.67-1.00 to 0.49-0.63. The tubes got only a little thinner, because the mould senses food and oats from less far.
Q2. It is scored by your share of all the food eaten (Dish::food_share). The star bars are 57% for one star, 62% for two, and none of the wild mould left for three (Win::Rival { bars: [0.57, 0.62] }).
Q3. It was first scored by walkers at the bell (1.5x / 3x / none left). Review 2 found that count swinging 28-54% for the player's mould in the last ten seconds, because four of the seven piles run dry together between about 2:10 and 2:20. The lead moved by up to half (for example seed 3: 7.6x at 140 s became 3.6x at the bell). Share of food eaten is cumulative, so a crash cannot move it.
Q4. Nothing. The snip alone against doing nothing is 5-3 (p 0.73), with 0 stars on all 8 seeds (share 0.44-0.59), because the cut heals. In review 2, three snips through the nest ("nestsnip") gave 0 stars on 8 of 8 (7-1, p 0.07). Under food scoring, the warden with and without the snip was 4-4 (exact hand) and 3-5 (clumsy hand), so no difference.
Q5. Doing nothing lives 1:08-3:48, median 2:10, over 16 seeds (0 stars on all 16).

Q7. The waterer (probe `sleeper`, using water) beats the oat-saver on 15 of 16 seeds (sign test p 0.0005): 2:51-10:42 (median 4:41) against 2:47-5:26 (median 3:34). In review 2, even the clumsiest water hand beat the oat-saver 8-0 (p 0.008).
Q8. The player holds 2 drops (WATER_STOCK), one comes back every 40 s (WATER_REGROW_SECONDS), and a drop has radius 12 cells (water_r).
Q9. The lamp dries the ground it shines on (dry_light 0.05 x light x wetness; ground rests at 0.5). Below crust_wet 0.25, walkers fall asleep (crust_rate 0.05 a step). A sleeper does not move or eat, pays no upkeep, is not pushed by the lamp, lays a faint trail (0.6), and dies at random with a mean life of 20 s unless ground wetter than 0.7 wakes it.
Q10. Walkers that only turn away from the light leave the lit stretch empty, and an empty stretch counts as a cut. Held on a tube for 3 s, the sensor lamp cut the food join on 5 of 5 seeds (weight 400, fade 0.25, burn), and every lighter setting also lost the join. The push lamp kept it joined on 5 of 5 when held on the tube. Held beside the tube (shifting it 5-7 cells), the push lamp kept 5 of 5 joined against the old lamp's 3 of 5.
Q11. Push is 0.9 (Params::push: the share of lit trail carried one cell outward per step, and the chance a lit walker is stepped outward). Reach is LAMP_R, 16 cells, at full brightness inside half of it.
Q12. 4 seconds (Params::wound 4.0), then the cut heals back to agar.
