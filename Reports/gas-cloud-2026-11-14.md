# Sleep gas as a cloud (2026-11-14)

Step 4 of the rounds' look plan ([research](noita-spell-look-2026-11-14.md)):
the sleep gas becomes a real field that drifts, after Noita's mists ("a
stationary cloud that stains the place and the creatures in it"). Before
this, a gas burst put the walkers in its disc to sleep in one frame and left
nothing in the world to see.

## What it does

- A gas burst still gasses its disc at once (`BigDish::gas`, unchanged). It
  also lays `KitRules::gas_dose` (1.0) of **cloud** on every agar cell in the
  disc (`BigDish::lay_gas`, the engine's `gaslay`).
- Each fine step the cloud gets the same 3x3 mean over the agar that the
  alarm and germ get, times `BigParams::gas_keep`. Walls and rock are off the
  agar, so they hold it. The kit sets the keep from a half-life of
  `KitRules::gas_half` 4 game seconds (`KitRules::gas_cloud`).
- Every living walker or sleeper on a cell with more cloud than
  `gas_sleep` (0.2) falls or stays asleep, with its timer at 0, gassed for at
  least `gas_linger` (3 s) more (`gas_sleepers`, the engine's `gasp`). So
  mould that walks into the cloud sleeps, and sleepers inside it stay asleep
  until it thins. Alarm still wakes a sleeper whose timer is 0, as before.
- The view shows the field itself: the engine's draw adds a violet haze,
  `1 - exp(-cloud / (2 gas_sleep))` of (45, 22, 90), over the ground and the
  mould in it. The first try at twice that strength washed the whole screen
  violet under a volley of gas rounds.

## Engine (GPU)

The cloud is the germ fields' third block (bindings 22/23 grow from 2 to 3
blocks of cells), spread in the trail pass before its early return, so shots
and the wipe leave it, as on the CPU. Params `_p0` becomes `gas_keep` (sent
only once a cloud is laid, so no tick spreads an empty one). The gas header
grows from 4 to 8 words; word 3 stays the explosion cards thread's, word 4
is the linger and word 5 the sleep level (`_p1` is the explosion thread's
`fling_dep`). `gasp` runs once a tick,
after `scent`, from the first cloud on.

`calm` used to stop looking once every gas word ran out (`gas_until`). With a
cloud it looks until a bound says the cloud is under `gas_sleep`, plus the
linger (`Engine::cloud_until`, from the cloud's top falling by at least
`gas_keep` a step).

The twin (`mouldgpu`, `the_gas_cloud_matches_the_cpu`) lays a cloud of
radius 8 on a plain dish and on one with dormancy and alarm, then runs 40
ticks on each engine with no re-inject. The results:

| | plain | dormancy and alarm |
|---|---|---|
| cloud cells off by over 1e-6 (each 10 ticks) | 0, 0, 0, 0 | 0, 0, 0, 0 |
| states off of 25,000 | 0, 0, 0, 0 | 0, 0, 0, 0 |
| gas words off | 0, 0, 0, 0 | 0, 0, 0, 0 |
| gassed sleepers (CPU) | 240, 246, 244, 202 | 212, 215, 209, 178 |

Two planted bugs fail it. A GPU cloud that sleeps walkers over 0.3 gives 18,
44, 87 and 178 gas words off. A GPU cloud that keeps 0.1% more a step puts
cells off. The squad twin (`a_gassed_mould_spares_the_squad...`) first
failed at frame 197 because loading a dish rewrote the gas header with
zeros, which lost the linger. `gas_words` now carries it.

Frame cost: one more 3x3 sum a cell in the trail pass and one pass over the
walkers a tick, but only once a cloud has been laid. This is not timed: the
cloud has only llvmpipe, and the 1070 is busy, so it goes in the C6 batch.
