Planet 8: the Dozer gets its own nap

Planet 8's slime, the Dozer, is the Swarmer that sleeps off every charge. It had been given the Sleeper's look from planet 6: a dark grey crust where it sleeps and gold where it wakes. So planet 8 read as planet 6, and on the core world's dark ground the grey looked like gravel. Now its sleeping walkers are a pale straw crust that keeps a little wet gloss, like a Fuligo drying to its rind, and the ones awake are the Swarmer's lemon and stand up into beads round its alarm. Every clip shows the same game on both sides, before on the left and after on the right. Only the view shader differs.

What changed

  • A new body look, SlimeLook::Doze. It uses the Sleeper's crust (the engine's life words, dormant_share) with its own rind: NAP_RGB (255, 240, 200) at NAP_DIM 0.95 of the body's brightness, losing only NAP_MATTE 0.2 of its gloss. The Sleeper's crust is 0.7 and matte 0.6.
  • Awake it takes the Swarmer's lemon (species_hue) and its swell, because the Dozer runs the Swarmer's lunge and last stand.
  • It shows in the slime viewer's body list as "doze", so it can be swapped there.
  • No cost: the same crust pass, with a colour picked by look.

Tried and dropped

  • A dark tan crust (255, 199, 128 at 0.75, matte 0.6). At rest the whole map is crust, and the tan read as bare dirt.
  • Two straws between the two. Neither read better at rest, and the darker one sat close to the ground.
  • Still open: at rest, any crust covering the whole view reads more as ground than as slime. The Sleeper has the same problem. The lemon waking through it is what makes the crust read as slime.
