Lap two: each tier with a second trait

If the run is 12 or 16 planets, lap two pairs each tier with a second trait that doesn't fight it. Each pair was smoke-tested for two minutes on its base tier's own map, run seed 1, alone and paired: does it run, does it do the named thing, and is it not a free win. One seed each; Enemy sets the numbers by play. Planet 8 is already the Swarmer that naps.

What fights, and how the pairs avoid it

  • Sleepers don't move in surges or the last stand: no sleeper takes the Swarmer's charge (planet 8 does, by Enemy's choice).
  • One alarm field: no sleeper takes the Sporer's burst or the Sprawler's fire. Planet 13 adds the alarm pull to the Sporer on purpose.
  • One life-cycle timer: no Sporer naps.
  • One scar: only 12 and 15 use it.
  • One walk per slime: no pair takes the Sprawler's walk.
  • The same trigger twice: the Trunk's setting and the Sporer's burst both answer a shot; not paired.
  • Planet 14's pump smell lands in the scent the Sleeper wakes on. That is the named thing, not a fight.
Recommendation

Use these seven as lap two's first draft. Each runs, does its thing in two minutes, and is no free win on one seed. Weakest in a pair: 13's last stand (not reached in 2:00) and 11's naps (it wakes at the first shot and stays up). Enemy sets the numbers on lap two's sizes.
