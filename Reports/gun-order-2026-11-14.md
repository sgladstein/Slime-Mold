# Arranging the gun in the shop (2026-11-14)

The owner, 20:32Z: "How do I actually build and adjust my guns. When I buy
a card it goes into the gun but I don't see how to move cards around".

## What was there

The shop had no way to order cards. A card bought went into the gun when it
fit, and a click took a card out or put a bag card back in. The gun then
sorted its cards: flight cards, then modifiers and triggers, then bullets
(`Run::gun`, sorted since the run mode, #280). So the firing order was out of the
player's hands, and the shop's slots looked like an order but were not one.

## What was built

- **The gun fires its cards in slot order, left to right** (`Run::gun` no
  longer sorts). The heading reads "In the gun, fired left to right" and
  each slot shows its number.
- **Drag a card onto a slot** to move it there; the cards between shift by
  one (`Pick::Move`, `StartEvent::Move`). A bag card dragged onto a slot goes
  in at that slot if the budget and slots allow. A card for sale dragged onto
  a slot is bought and put there (`Run::buy_into`), or into the bag when the
  gun has no room. A gun card dragged onto the bag area comes out. Let go
  anywhere else, or on its own slot, and nothing happens. A press that moves
  less than `DRAG_FROM` (6 px) is still a click.
- While dragging, the card rides under the pointer. Its old place shows as a
  hole, and the target slot gets an accent ring with a bar on its left edge.
  Over the bag, the whole bag area is ringed.
- **Q and E** move the focused card in the gun one slot sooner or later, and
  the keyboard focus goes with it.
- A card that is bought or clicked in without a slot still lands where the
  sort used to put it (`Run::put_in`): after the cards of its rank and before
  the first card of a later rank. So a bought Wide still widens the first
  bullet, and nothing changes for a player who never drags.
- When a smaller gun is carried, the last card in the gun that is not a
  bullet comes out first, then the last bullet (`Run::refit`). This keeps the
  gun with something to fire; before this change, the last card bought came
  out first.
- The detail box, the hint line and each card's text say how to move cards.

## Films

These are made with `larder-world look=DIR run=5 menu=shop-mid
screen=1600x900` and the new `drag=FROM>TO[/FROM>TO..]`. FROM is a slot
number counted from 1, or a card's name. TO is a slot or `bag`. The drag
goes through the menu's own mouse code, and the window applies it to the
run as it would in play. An arrow is drawn on the frames where the pointer
was. Sixty frames at 15 per second, cropped to the armory.

- `reorder.gif` (`drag=Smother>1/3>1`): Smother from the bag into slot 1,
  then Needle gas from slot 3 to slot 1. The log reads `the gun fires
  smother+bomb+gas`, then `gas+smother+bomb`.
- `buy-and-out.gif` (`drag=Wide>1/1>bag`): Wide for sale dragged onto slot
  1, bought (budget 12 of 12, 80 points) and put in first, then dragged to
  the bag (out).

  Index-based picks moved because `put_in` reorders, the refit now drops Wide
  instead of Thick, and a hand-set gun now fires `blast+wide+pierce` in its
  own order instead of the sort's `pierce+wide+blast`.

## Frame cost

The shop page draws while the game is paused in the menu, so this adds no
per-step cost. A drag draws one extra card and a ring.
