Larder World, heard

A shot now sounds as big as what it killed. Each shot is a crack where the soldier stands and an impact where it struck. The impact's weight is the walkers that shot killed, so a shot into a thick vein is a heavy, low, long slap and a graze is a light tap. Every walker killed is also one pop in the kill crackle.

Everything here is a count the game already has; nothing is played for the ear alone. These were rendered offline by the code the window runs. Headphones help: sounds sit left or right of the screen where they happen, and quieter the farther off screen they are.

What I need from your ears

  1. The shot ladder: does a big kill feel like a big hit, and is the step from light to heavy enough?
  2. The rush: does the pulse help you feel the mould closing in, or does it get in the way?
  3. Levels: are the shots too loud or too quiet against the rest?
  4. On the PC, press O in Larder World to turn sound off and on.
