# Bug sweep after the squad-mode playtest (2026-11-14)

The owner's playtest of `larder-world mode=squads`, 08:01Z: "the gun
animation gets messed up sometimes. The soliders are constantly shuffeling
around in their formation. My unit got stuck and cannot move anymore. The
shortcuts for harvesting and maybe other things doesn't work. I cannot zoom
in anymore". At 08:12Z: "It is squad mode the messes up the zoom".

Each bug was reproduced in a test or a headless probe before it was
fixed. This report keeps one section per fix: the first two landed in
PR #252, the window's three after it.

## A squad stuck for good against rock or the map's edge

**What was wrong.** A steered or sent squad has an anchor its soldiers
stand round (`Game::squad_turn`), which waits once it is more than `LEAD`
(4 cells) ahead of the squad's middle. Steered into rock or the map's edge,
`Game::guide` found no walking map from the far point (it was in the rock)
and went straight on, into the rock or out past the edge. The soldiers
stopped at the edge, the anchor got more than `LEAD` ahead of them, and it
then waited for them for good: steering the other way, or a right click,
moved it 0 cells. Steered for 25 s in 8 headings on seeds 1-4 of the race
world, 15 of 32 squads never moved again (the middle moved 0.0-0.2 cells in
the 4 s steered back).

**The fix.** The anchor never leaves standable ground (`Game::slide`: else
along one axis, as a soldier walks, else it stays), an anchor or a dest off
the ground comes back to the nearest ground, and an anchor that is ahead
still steps when the step brings it back toward the middle. 0 of 32 stuck;
`a_squad_steered_into_rock_or_the_edge_comes_back_out` keeps 24 of them.

Also: the walking-map cache is capped at `MAPS_MAX` (48) maps. A map is
the whole world (512 x 320 fine cells at the window's scale 2, 655 KB,
about 15 ms to build; 295 ms at 2048 x 1280), and a squad's goals move with
its anchor, so a squad pressed against rock could add one every step and
keep them all for `PATHS_EVERY` (30 s). Steered round the race world for
30 s, a squad built 2-21 maps on seeds 1-3, so the cap only bites in the
pressed case.

## Soldiers shuffling in their formation

**What was wrong.** A soldier's place in its squad's formation
(`Game::place`) was ranked by its index among the members and turned with
the squad's facing, which for the controlled squad follows the cursor
(`Cmd::Face`, sent whenever the cursor moves). Moving the mouse turned a
resting blob round its middle and every soldier walked to a new place; a
death renumbered everyone behind it.

**Measure.** Cells walked by an 8-soldier squad in 5 s, summed over seeds
1-4, at rest, with the cursor circling it once (as the window sends
`Face`), and after one member dies:

| formation | at rest | cursor circling | after a death |
|---|---|---|---|
| blob, before | 0.0 | 850.5 | 241.3 |
| blob, now | 0.0 | 0.0 | 35.2 |
| wedge, before | 0.0 | 1049.9 | 216.3 |
| wedge, now | 0.0 | 992.8 | 47.9 |
| line, before | 0.0 | 938.6 | 66.8 |
| line, now | 0.0 | 929.8 | 62.9 |

**The fix.** A blob and a ring look the same whichever way they face, so
they no longer turn (`turned`). Every squad's members are matched to its
places each step, nearest pair first (`Game::rank_squad`: round the anchor
while it moves, round the point they were all sent to while it rests), so a
formation that turns, grows or loses a member moves each soldier as little
as it can. A line or wedge still turns to face the cursor, as it should: a
full turn of a line of 8 walks its ends about 55 cells each, so most of its
930 cells is the turn itself. `a_formation_holds_its_places_when_the_cursor_circles_or_a_member_falls`
keeps the blob and wedge numbers.

## The gun animation

**What was wrong.** A GPU step runs across frames (`Game::begin_step`,
then `finish_step` a frame or two later), and every command finishes the
step in flight first (`Game::apply`). The window took a step's notes (each
bullet's leg, the muzzle flash, the way the gun points, the rounds' casts)
only when it finished the step itself, so a step a command finished was
never seen: its bullets flew with no leg or flash. In the squad mode the
cursor sent a `Fire` (while the button is held) or a `Face` whenever it
moved 0.75 cells, almost every frame.

**Measure.** The fork map's squad (seed 1, scale 1, 1250 walkers) with the
cursor circling it once every 4 s for 15 s, the left button held from 5 s
to 10 s:

| | steps a command finished early | shots fired | bullet legs drawn |
|---|---|---|---|
| before | 222 of about 225 | 40 | 0 |
| now | 0 of about 225 | 40 | 200 |

Before, nearly every step was finished by the cursor's own command, so not
one of the 40 shots drew a leg or a flash: the bullets hit with no
animation. (4 soldiers, the camera held on them; 188 legs drew while the
button was held and 12 more as the last bullets landed.) The facing change
below is a look change and was not counted.

**Also.** Between shots a soldier's figure faced the way it last moved, by
any move over 0.05 fine cells, so the shoves of elbow room and a
formation settling spun the guns round between shots.

**The fix.** `App::catch_up` takes the notes of any step finished since
the window last looked (by a command, a tool or the card), and the squad
mode's aim waits for the boundary between steps (`SquadInput::aim`, just
before `begin_step`): the volley point moves once the cursor has moved
0.75 cells, and a line or wedge turns once the cursor has swung 0.17 rad
round the squad. The controlled squad's guns point at the cursor and a
volleying squad's at its volley point; any other soldier turns to the way
it walks once it has walked `TURN` (0.3 cells) since it last turned.
`a_step_a_command_finishes_keeps_its_shots` fails without `catch_up` (0
legs drawn of 1).

## The shortcuts in the squad mode

**What was wrong.** The squad's own keys were asked before the command
card's, so G merged squads instead of harvesting, X split instead of
Defend, and T cycled the formation even on the build page. The card's
WASD letters (A attack-move, S stop, W sow; the tools' D mine and S
gas) steered instead.
The card's orders went soldier by soldier, and a squad on the move gave
its members its own order back at its next step. In the RTS round the
Rally point sat at (2, 2), under the build page's button, so only R
reached it. And the squad's keys were nowhere in the game: the F1 card
listed the RTS controls, the start menu's Keys page six lines at the
bottom (the owner, 08:19Z: "how do you split the squad?", "that should be
in the menu, also not mentioned in controls").

**The fix.** WASD and Space come first; every other key goes to the card
first, then to the squad. Split moved to **Y** and join to **J** (the
owner had agreed X and G at 04:18Z, before the card's letters clashed;
moving them back is a two-line change). The card's letters move off WASD
in this mode: attack-move and the mine to R, the gas to F, sowing to U;
Stop and the rally point go (letting go of WASD stops the squad; there is
no larder). Harvest reads "Drain a crystal", and the card's drain,
attack-move and stop go to the controlled squad as one
(`Cmd::SquadOrder`). F1 in the squad mode opens on the squad's keys, and
the start menu's Keys page lists them as a section of their own (first
when `mode=squads`). The Rally point is at (1, 3); with the player's own
mould, whose Sow takes that slot, the page is full and R still reaches it.
`the_squad_modes_keys_do_what_the_card_says` checks each card letter, the
split key, the F1 rows and the wheel.

## The zoom in the squad mode

**What was wrong.** Three things. The wheel switched squads, so zooming
needed Ctrl, which nothing said. Zoomed at the cursor, the camera moved,
which the follow read as a pan by the player, so it stopped following.
And the follow itself drifted: it eased from the screen's middle
(`Camera::centre`), but `centre_on` puts a point in the middle of the
band between the HUD's insets (32 px at the top, 112 at the bottom), so
each frame fell short and the camera came to rest about 100 cells below
the squad (clamped at the map's edge). Steered right for 5 s with two
zooms in, the squad's middle ended 76 cells from the view's middle,
off the top of the screen at zoom 8.

**The fix.** The wheel zooms about the squad while the camera follows it
(`App::squad_zoom`), Ctrl + wheel switches squads, and the follow eases
from `Camera::focus`, the inverse of `centre_on`. Same run: at most 1.6
cells off, the squad in the middle of the view
(`the_squad_cameras_follow_keeps_the_squad_in_the_middle`).
