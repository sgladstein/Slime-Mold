Shove cards

You asked for ways to change the slug's pushback, and for a gun that can corral the slime into a kill zone. Three new cards make a bullet's hit shove the slime somewhere: Heave straight on, Corral toward your nearest turret, Pull back toward the gun. Every clip is the game window's own frames on software Vulkan (the Fork, eight soldiers holding at the larder, squad mode). All clips are one game up to 29 s with plain bullets. At 29 s a turret stands up and to the right of the squad, off its line of fire, and every squad loads the clip's cards, so the clips differ only in those cards over the next 3 s.

How they work

  • Heave (cost 2): the hit shoves the slime round it on along the bullet's line. Each heave throws it 1.5 times farther.
  • Corral (cost 2): the shove heads for your nearest standing turret within 40 cells of the hit. With no turret in reach it goes straight on.
  • Pull (cost 1): the shove heads back toward the soldier who fired.
  • On a slug: the cards steer the slug's own shove, so a corral slug herds the slug's whole throw.
  • What you see is the slime itself: every thrown walker is a real walker in flight that lands and lives on, drawn as a hot speck with a streak.
Every clip loops. Slow motion makes it easy to follow where each shove sends the slime.

Not settled

  • Pull is a trap as built. It drags the slime onto the squad: all eight soldiers were dead in 10 s. It should stop short of the gun, or be dropped. Your call.
  • Corral only finds turrets. It could aim at a spot you mark, a wall's face, or any building.
  • Every shove card costs the squad kills. The shove trades kills for where the slime ends up. That is a job for the balance pass.
  • Frame cost on the GTX 1070 is not measured. A shove is the same burst a slug's shove already was. It adds no new GPU pass and needs one read-back per shove, which the plan's batching step covers.
