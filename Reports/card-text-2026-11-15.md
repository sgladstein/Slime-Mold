# Simpler gun-card text (2026-11-15)

The owner, 02:57Z: "The text for all the gun cards is too much. They should
be simplified and clarified".

## What was there

- **The HUD's card tooltip** gave each card one to three sentences, then
  "Costs N of the gun's budget". A rolled bullet gave its numbers, a
  standard bullet's damage and its hole's width beside them, then what its
  payload does.
- **The shop's detail box** had more on top of that:
  - the card's kind and budget, then its words;
  - for a rolled bullet, its numbers twice;
  - then how to click and drag it ("Slot 1 of the gun, fired in turn: click
    to take it out, drag it to another slot (or Q and E) to move it").
- **The Needle bomb text** ran past the box.
- **The kind labels** were jargon: Payload, Modifier, Trigger and Flight.
- **The guns** gave a long sentence plus a line of numbers that the stat
  bars already show.

## What changed

- **Each card is one short sentence**, with only the number that matters,
  at most 60 characters (`draw::card_face`):
  - "Kills slime within 6 cells and leaves a crater."
  - "Its hit is 1.6x wider."
  - "Bullets go through one slime hit."
  - "Fires 2x as often."
- **The kinds say what a card works on** (`CardKind::label`):
  - Bullet (was Payload)
  - Next bullet (was Modifier and Trigger)
  - Always (was Flight)
- **The shop** puts the kind before the words, and the budget last
  (`draw::card_words`), e.g. "Next bullet: its hit is 1.6x wider. Budget 2."
  The HUD tooltip shows the kind in its pill instead.
- **A rolled bullet** shows one line of numbers, then what it does:
  - `draw::ammo_numbers`: "Damage 5.5, speed 2.0x, range 1.7x, spread 0.3x."
  - then, for example, "Kills slime within 4 cells and throws the rest 10
    cells out."
  - The standard bullet's numbers are no longer shown beside it.
- **How to click and drag** is no longer on every card. It is only on the
  hint line and in the box's default text. A card's slot is now its state
  ("slot 1"), shown at the box's top right.
- **Guns** get a short line each (`GunKind::about`), such as "Slow and far.
  A big budget in few slots." The stat bars carry the numbers.
- **`Card::about`**, the words without numbers, is cut down the same way.
- **The idle note** reads "No bullet after it: it does nothing".

`card-text-2026-11-15/`.

## Tests

`the_loadout_panel_lights_the_next_cast_and_dims_dead_cards` now checks
every fixed card's words:

- one sentence, at most 60 characters;
- no "payload", "modifier", "trigger", "walker" or "mould";
- the kind is one of the three new labels;
- a rolled bullet's line starts "Damage" and stays within 120 characters;
- `card_words` reads as above.

The run's shop test checks a gun card's state ("slot 2") and its plain
words.
