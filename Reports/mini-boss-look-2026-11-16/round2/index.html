Mini-boss Kinds Look

The four mini-boss kinds, each in its own look. It is the same boss in the same run each time, forced to one kind (planet 3 of run 1, formed at 1:25, squad holding). The colour, the dome, the glow and the dots all come from its own walkers. When one breaks up or runs out, its look drains back into the slime as its walkers let go.

Would you swap any of these?

Each kind's look is a first pick, chosen so the four differ in brightness as well as colour. The hunter's violet is still open in the round-1 A/B (A, B or C). If you pick B there, the hunter takes the crimson and the sapper gets another look.

The four kinds

Zoom 12, camera on the boss. The hunter clip runs at 2x game speed, the others at 5x to 10x. Counts are from the game's boss lines, second by second.

The leech rings its pump

The leech now holds its walkers in a band round the rig, 14 to 18 cells from the pump, instead of under it. Spread that thin through the thick slime round a pump, it showed only as dark flecks in a gold collar. Now a boss out on its errand shows wherever its own walkers stand, however thick the slime around them, so the ring reads as dark segments round the rig, turning blue as it drinks. A boss still forming keeps the old look, its colour seeping through the slime.

When one breaks up or runs out

The sapper runs out at 3:02 with all 196 walkers. Its walkers are let go over 1.5 s, and its light goes out first, then its colour and its dome sink back into the slime as they spread. Before, it would have snapped back to gold in one frame.

What changed since round 1

  • Each kind has its own look: dark (leech), middle (hunter, sapper) and pale (wanderer), so they part by brightness as well as colour.
  • The fade comes from the game's own break-up: the look drains while the walkers are still marked as the boss's.
  • The leech's glow is its take: what it has drunk divided by (that + 150).
  • The glow is softer. A boss that sits still packs its walkers tightly, and the old glow washed the crimson to salmon and the pale one to white. The skin is the colour you see now, and the walkers' own dots carry the bright points.

The walkers move exactly as in the mini-boss thread's round-3 films: the boss lines match second for second. Frame cost on the GTX 1070 is still to be measured.
