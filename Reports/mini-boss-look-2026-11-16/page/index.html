Today, for comparison

What drives each part of the picture

  • Its colour spreads by the share of walkers in a spot that belong to the hunter. While it gathers inside the gold body you see its colour seep through the sheet; once it is out on its own it is solid.
  • Its height and gloss come from how thickly its own members stand. It rises as a wet dome where they are thick, with a darker membrane where they thin out at the edge.
  • The light inside it comes from where its members are thickest. A body that bunches up glows brighter there. In the clips it is fairly even, because today's hunter is an even cloud of about half a walker per cell. The lunge and clench coming in the mini-boss engine will make it bunch for real.
  • The dots are its walkers, one each. A dot is brighter when that walker has more energy, so a hunter far from food dims as it runs down. A dot turns cold white as that walker takes hits, so you can see where your fire lands.
  • Strength: a stronger hunter stands taller and its core burns brighter. Every hunter in these clips is the same grade for now. Grades arrive in the mini-boss thread's next change.
