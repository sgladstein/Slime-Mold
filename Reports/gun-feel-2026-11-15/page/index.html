A bolder spray, camera shake and hit-stop

You saw no difference in the spray A/B, and the frames agreed: the two versions differed in 5 of 30 frames. The droplets were drawn as ordinary walker specks, which the slime body dims to almost nothing. They now have their own layer. Below that are the camera shake and the hit-stop you asked to see, each next to the same moment with it off.

Every clip is real time at 30 frames a second, zoom 12. Left is off or today's; right is the new one.

The spray, bolder (on by default)

Each walker a shot kills is still a real walker flying out of the hit, and the kills are the same. It is now a bigger droplet with a dark rim, white-hot while it is fast and cooling to deep orange as it lands. It flies 0.45 s and up to 7 cells (it was 0.3 s and 5). Slime thrown by a bomb keeps its gold, with the same rim.

Camera shake (off unless turned on)

Each blast shakes the camera by its size: what it kills and throws. Plain hits don't shake it, because in a fight every volley would. The HUD stays still. These are 10 seconds of eight soldiers firing bombs on the Fork, 30 to 40 s.

Hit-stop (off unless turned on)

When a big blast lands, the game holds still for a moment (0.05 to 0.12 s by its size, at most once every 0.6 s). The flash and the flying slime hold too. The right side falls a little behind the left as the holds add up.

What's next

The bolder spray goes in now, on by default. Shake and hit-stop stay off. If you want either one in the game, say which, and I'll turn it on by default and give it a switch in Options.
