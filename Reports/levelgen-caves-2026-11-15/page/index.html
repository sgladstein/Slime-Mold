Cave Tunnels

The planet reviews found the caves read as a node graph, round caverns joined by tubes of one width, and suspected they were a slog at 4x. Now the caverns are lobed, every tunnel pinches and widens along its way, and each cave planet has a few big rooms to fight in away from the landing and the nests. Every picture pairs main (before) with this change (after): same planet, same seed, same moment.

Tunnel widths12-17 → 9-22dish cells, 10th to 90th percentile along the tunnels' middles, 20 caves. Pinches never under 8; the widest about 25.
Room to fight in81 → 134map cells from rock at the middle of the widest room away from the landing and nests, the median of 20 caves (76-109 → 120-235). Four rooms on the caves' 4x map.
What it costs19 → 19first-try builds of 20. Build 362 → 438 ms, once a level. Rock 51% → 44.5% of the map; crystals 31 → 35 (medians).

Widths are in dish cells, the units the soldiers and slime move in (a map cell is half of one). The landing, the nests and the regions stay where they were; the caverns, tunnels and crystals are rolled again.

Tried on the way

  • Pinches down to 6 dish cells. Crystals were cut off on 17 of 20 caves' first tries, 5 fell back to the open plan, and a build took 5.6 s on average. The planet's check (and a squad) counts a gap under about 8 dish cells as shut, so pinches stop at 8.
