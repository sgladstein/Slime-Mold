# Things to try: at most five lines, shown in the game beside "What is new",
# each with a tick the game remembers on that PC. The Direction lane owns
# this file; other lanes ask Direction for a line. One thing a line, in
# plain words, the most important first. Lines starting with # are notes.
Hearts: a heart grows on any crystal the slime keeps packed thick for two minutes. Break it to take that crystal back; you win a planet only by wiping out every last bit of slime. Is it clear what to go for?
After each won planet, pick the next on the star map: Safe, Fair or Hard, the harder ones paying more, with bonus goals on each. Was the choice a real one, and is the run too hard, too easy, or about right?
Each planet's slime tells you on landing what it does to you, and a small pill in the top row tracks it. Did you notice it, and did it change how you played?
Quit in the middle of a planet, then pick Back to the run: it carries on where you left it. Does it come back as you left it?
Turrets are bought like guns: a gun turret, a lobber, a sprayer and a hunter, each with its own loadout, and up to three marked Quick build on the Build page. Did you build more of them than before?
