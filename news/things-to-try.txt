# each with a tick the game remembers on that PC. The Direction lane owns
# this file; other lanes ask Direction for a line. One thing a line, in
# plain words, the most important first. Lines starting with # are notes.
The heart: each planet's slime now has a heart on its home crystal. Kill it to win the planet, or drain its crystal to stop it regrowing. Is it clear from the start what to go for?
Planets 2 to 8 are harder: raids come sooner and tougher, mini-bosses reach you, strikers grow with a big army. Too hard, too easy, or about right?
Each planet's slime tells you on landing what it does to you, and a small amber number under the goal line tracks it. Did you notice it, and did it change how you played?
Quit in the middle of a planet, then pick Back to the run: it carries on where you left it. Does it come back as you left it?
Two new turrets in the Build tab after Mortars: the Sprayer, which lays its cards in a strip, and the Hunter, which snipes the slime on your crystals. Do either earn their price?
